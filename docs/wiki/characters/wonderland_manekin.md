# Wonderland Manekin

## Overview

A Manekin gifted to you and Paimon by a witch who uses the pseudonym "Octavia." They are gifted at exploring the mysteries sealed within the Miliastra Wonderland.
The
Wonderland Manekin
is a playable
character
in
Genshin Impact
, serving as the primary playable character of the
Miliastra Wonderland
gameplay mode.
After unlocking Miliastra Wonderland, players will gain access to two versions of the Manekin:
Manekin
(male) and
Manekina
(female). The Manekins can be named, customized with
Cosmetic Plans
, and used in Miliastra Wonderland and
Teyvat
.
In Miliastra Wonderland, players can freely swap between the Manekin and the Manekina at any time. In Teyvat, players have both Manekins simultaneously as separate characters but they cannot both be used in the same team. The Manekin can be used in the open world and most domains, but not in gameplay modes such as the
Spiral Abyss
,
Imaginarium Theater
, and
Stygian Onslaught
. Their
element
is set to the
Traveler
's current element.
