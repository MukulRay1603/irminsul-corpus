# Columbina

## Storyline

Columbina Hyposelenia
, also known as the
Moon Maiden
and by her former
Fatui
codename
The Damselette
, is the
Moon Goddess
worshiped by the
Frostmoon Scions
in
Nod-Krai
. She was born on
Hiisi Island
500 years ago, and was subsequently adopted by the Frostmoon Scions as their
god
under the name Kuutar. She eventually chose to leave the Frostmoon Scions after growing disillusioned with them, and instead joined the
Fatui
, where she became the Third of the
Eleven Fatui Harbingers
. After realizing that they only sought her power, she left the Fatui and began hiding in the
Silvermoon Hall
at
Hiisi Island
to rest and recover her strength.
She was eventually found in Silvermoon Hall by the
Traveler
, whom she later befriended. Soon afterwards,
Rerir
managed to revive himself in a physical body, and began to hunt her, though he was eventually defeated. After being captured by
Dottore
, in order to deny him usage of the Three Moons' powers, she chose to enter a Moon Gate, which transported her to the Lunar Palace. As time flows backwards within the Lunar Palace, she was able to meet the Three Moon Sisters who had been imprisoned there by
Nibelung
during the
war of funerary flame
. After the Sisters agreed to transfer their authorities to her, she became the Trilune Goddess with the powers and authority of all Three Moons, despite two of the Moons being shattered. During the final battle against Dottore, she was revived in the present day through the assistance of her friends, and together they defeated him with her newfound powers.
Profile
Columbina is the Trilune Goddess, who possesses the Trilunar Authority that Nibelung bestowed to the Three Moons to govern
Teyvat
.
She was originally the Goddess of the
Frost Moon
,
but was granted the power of all Three Moons when the original Three Goddesses transferred their authorities of using the Three Moons to her.
She is worshiped as the Moon Maiden Kuutar by the Frostmoon Scions,
who acknowledge her as the New Moon and successor of
Canon
.
They pray to her for protection, and they have carved multiple statues across
Nod-Krai
in her image, called "
Statues of the New Moon
."
She was formerly the Third of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, where she operated under the name Columbina and codename The Damselette.
Columbina was never sent on any missions as a Harbinger, and was kept around by the
Tsaritsa
primarily for her powers as a Moon Goddess. She generally had cordial relationships with the other Harbingers, primarily
Sandrone
,
Arlecchino
, and
Signora
,
while she also participated in Sandrone's tea parties with
Tartaglia
and
Capitano
.
Tartaglia was wary of her due to her high ranking despite rarely displaying her power,
while the
Wanderer
regarded her as unfeeling and unconcerned with her surroundings.
As a Moon Goddess, Columbina has the ability to use the power of the Light Realm.
In battle, Columbina's power manifests in a form similar to
Hydro
.
Kuuvahki
is the manifestation of Columbina's body, given that she was composed of the
Primordial Waters
which gave her life and the Frost Moon's light. This makes Kuuvahki a Hydro-based light, similar to the Moonfall Silver that accompanied her birth, which is Hydro under elemental sight. She rained down Kuuvahki in Nod-Krai during the
War of Funerary Flame
when she was trapped within the Moon's Reflection. Due to the Frost Moon being sealed beyond the
false sky
, as well as due to her innate existence being rejected by the planet,
Columbina was originally unable to access her full potential power as a Moon Goddess.
Nonetheless, she still held power comparable to that of other
gods
.
After fulfilling an unspecified agreement with the
Tsaritsa
, she lost much of her strength;
however, after drawing upon the energy within the
Moon Marrows
possessed by the
Traveler
and
Arlecchino
, she restored her power to a level beyond what she originally had as the Third Harbinger in preparation to fight
Rerir
,
before exhausting it.
After being granted the authorities of the other Moon Sisters, she now possesses the Trilune Authority. This grant her right to use marrows legitimately, though after she has chosen to part with a portion of it to restore Nod-Krai's natural supply of Kuuvahki.
After leaving the Fatui, Columbina has taken up residence at the
Silvermoon Hall
in
Hiisi Island
, where she periodically receives visits from the Traveler and
Paimon
.
Personality
Due to her isolated past both from her time in the Frostmoon Scions and in the Fatui, Columbina has very poor social skills, that translate on her being seen as mysterious and weird by others. According to
Tartaglia
and Scaramouche (as his current incarnation as the
Wanderer
), Columbina is supposedly a dangerous, yet enigmatic being, with both warning the Traveler to be cautious around her. However,
Arlecchino
finds her an interesting and special being.
She has little to no filter when it comes to interacting with other people, commenting on
Dottore's
youthful appearance, much to his dismay, and jokingly saying that
Sandrone
should be drinking oil instead of coffee, what led her to being cursed out by her fellow Harbinger (a memory that Columbina is not too fond of remembering). This characteristic, added to her innocent demeanor, gives her a snarky and amusing personality. Nonetheless, Columbina appreciates those she considers her friends, having been taught numerous lessons from them.
Despite being the current reincarnation of the Frost Moon Goddess, Columbina has some resentment to being treated as one, because the Frostmoon Scions from old used to ask for more than she could give, which was what made her eventually join the Fatui, only to have her powers used for their goals. Due to not feeling like she truly belonged with either the Frostmoon Scions or the Fatui, she did not identify with any of the numerous names and titles that they had bestowed upon her.
Consequently, she told the Traveler that they may address her with whichever name they want.
Later on, she eventually came to accept "Columbina" as the name used for her by her newfound friends, ultimately choosing to adopt "Columbina Hyposelenia" as her real name.
Another trait of Columbina is her strong connection with nature; she is able to communicate with the kuuhenki, and the flowers in Silvermoon Hall change color according to her current state: when she is angry or feels unsafe, the flowers turn red, and when she is weakened, they turn white. She also prefers food closest to their natural state, such as fresh berries, bread, and vegetables, and dislikes strong spirits such as Fire-Water.
Appearance
See also:
Moonweave Gossamer
Columbina uses the medium female model. She has pale skin and long black hair with dark pink/magenta highlights, tied into two side-plaits resting just over her shoulders with white ribbon around it in an X-shape. Most of the time, she keeps her eyes closed, though when opened, they are revealed to be a light pink and nearly pearl-like color, with blurry light blue pupils and a crescent-shaped highlight under it.
She leaves behind white feathers that quickly dissipates when she teleports herself or other people. In her second fight against Rerir, she leaves behind a trail of small constellations that is similar to
Paimon
's when she moves.
History
Pre-Fatui Life
Kuutar was born from Frost
Moonlight
and the
Primordial Sea
, within the
Sanctum of the Oathbearer
around five hundred years ago,
in fulfillment of a prophecy stating that a new Goddess of the
Frost Moon
would one day be born.
She does not remember the circumstances of her birth, aside from a particular song that was sung, which she would hum periodically. Her birth was witnessed by
Aila
, and she subsequently agreed to live with the Frostmoon Scions.
During her time with the Frostmoon Scions, they felt entitled to her power and cared little about her as a person, instead pressuring her into granting their prayers by giving her unwanted offerings.
Fatui Recruitment
Over a century ago, Kuutar came to believe that she could no longer satisfy the demands of the Frostmoon Scions and decided to leave them.
Several decades afterwards, she joined the Fatui at
Snezhnaya
, where the
Tsaritsa
offered her a "second home".
Abandoning Kuutar, she was made into a Harbinger, and was given the name "Columbina" and codename "The Damselette" by the Tsaritsa.
While serving in the Fatui, unlike many of her colleagues, she was never sent to retrieve
Gnoses
from the
Archons
of the other six nations, but was simply kept in Snezhnaya without much to do aside from helping
Dottore
with
kuuvahki
-related projects, though she stopped working with him after sensing that he had malevolent intent and would try to avoid him when possible. In addition, when she asked the Tsaritsa when she can start the task assigned to her, she was told that "the time has not yet come".
Nonetheless, she would appreciate some of her colleagues' company, particularly
Sandrone
. When she met
Arlecchino
, she used to creep up on her as a means of "testing" her. Columbina tried to do the same to her but was unable to until she used kuuvahki, which appeared to have frightened her to the point that she no longer did so. She also learned about Arlecchino's status as a Crimson Moon Dynasty descendant. She also had some interactions with
Signora
, who taught her to express gratitude to others.
At some point after
Scaramouche
obtained the
Electro
Gnosis and cut off contact from the Fatui,
she and her fellow Harbingers attended
Rosalyne
's funeral. After the ceremony ended, she complimented Dottore's youthful appearance and asked him about the "segment in the prime of his life."
After
Capitano
’s merger with
Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night
, Columbina, along with Dottore and Sandrone, were dispatched to Nod-Krai to work on a project.
Around this time, the Tsaritsa called upon Columbina to fulfill the task she assigned to her, which used up much of Columbina's divine power.
Post-Fatui Life
After realizing the Fatui were no different from the Frostmoon Scions that used to worship her, and believing she had no more obligations to the Fatui, Columbina left the organization and returned to Hiisi Island. The subordinates originally under her command were transferred to Dottore.
There, she met
Lauma
, who apologized on behalf of her ancestors and had her hide in
Silvermoon Hall
to rest and recover her strength from completing the Tsaritsa's task.
The Fatui, disapproving of her departure, issued the "Palestar Edict"—the highest of the Tsaritsa's commands—in order to recover her at any cost.
While most rank-and-file Fatui appeared to prioritize the order, Columbina's colleagues were not as interested.
Columbina would later meet the Traveler after they were brought to her by a
Luonnotar
. She answered a few questions for them in the process, though she became displeased when the Traveler asked about the Fatui, prompting the Traveler to drop the subject.
She later helped them recover a memory of the
spaceship
they used to arrive on Teyvat. Upon learning of the Wild Hunt, Columbina told them that their enemy was the "Rächer of Solnari," who the two recognized as
Rerir
. She told them she had only learned of this name from her colleagues.
During a battle against Rerir on
Starsand Shoal
, Columbina used her power to remotely teleport the Traveler, Paimon,
Flins
, Sandrone, and
Pulonia
to her residence in the Silvermoon Hall before he could kill them, resulting in Sandrone criticizing her for interrupting their fight.
Sandrone eventually left with Pulonia on her own accord, while Flins thanked her for their rescue.
As the Traveler and Paimon left, she clutched her chest, realizing that she didn't have enough time left.
Later, she met with the Traveler and Paimon in
Nasha Town
, since the Fatui were too busy and her colleagues likely had no interest in capturing her.
She also believed that someone or something was intentionally hiding their spaceship and expressed a desire to return to the moon to find answers.
A while after these events, the Traveler would meet with Columbina, learning more about her past. They helped a kuuhenki,
Maalaus
, get some materials for painting, who created art of Columbina when she was worshiped by the Frostmoon Scions.
Later, another kuuhenki's lyre had broken, so Columbina agreed to have the Traveler head to an artisan's house with her to get it repaired. They quickly discerned that the artisan was
Aino
, who was terrified upon seeing the lyre as she had often seen it appear mysteriously and disappear just as mysteriously after she repaired it. While Aino was fixing the lyre, Columbina inexplicably appeared and gave the Traveler some trinkets to give as a sign of thanks, only for
Ineffa
to walk in on them. Columbina quickly hid behind the Traveler, while Ineffa recognized her as the Moon Goddess from the likenesses of the Statues of the New Moon. Aino failed to recognize her, but thanked her for the trinkets she gave and that she was welcome to return any time.
Columbina was later decorating the field in the hall with the kuuhenki when the Traveler stopped by to visit her, learning that she needed more
Rainbowdrop Crystals
to finish her temporary mural. While gathering the stones, they came upon a lost child from the Frostmoon Scions; not wanting to scare her, Columbina used the stones to create a path for the child to point the way home.
As Columbina is having an afternoon nap, her knee was bumped by a male
Chic Badger
, who was found at the entrance of her cave by the Traveler and Paimon. While revealing that she can also talk to animals, she relates that the badger was being chased by monsters when he was searching for Lauma. They then went to the
Frostmoon Enclave
where Lauma is doing some requests from her people. While waiting, Columbina learned that the motto has changed since she last heard it in the past when Lauma said "May the moonlight connect us". Upon meeting and leaving the badger with Lauma, Columbina expressed gratitude that the Scions have changed.
Rerir would later attack Columbina, and even with the Traveler, Flins and Lauma helping her, they were easily overpowered by him.
Rerir fired a projectile at
Jahoda
, but Columbina managed to shove her out of the way while taking a minor injury in the process.
Since they could not fight him, she agreed with Jahoda's suggestion to run.
Upon meeting with the Northern Intelligence Network, Columbina decided to visit the
Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau
and asked Sandrone to help her arrange a meeting with Arlecchino, as she would have access to the Iridescent Moon Marrow which would help further restore her strength.
When Arlecchino arrived, she saved her and the Traveler from the Wild Hunt and later helped them retrieve the Iridescent Moon Marrow from the Crimson Moon subspace, restoring Columbina's power to the level it was back when she was in the Fatui; in return, Columbina entrusted the Iridescent Moon Marrow to Arlecchino.
Columbina remarked that it was still not enough to overpower Rerir, but a plan concieved by and carried out by
Albedo
with
Durin
's assistance using alchemy allowed her to siphon power from him and expel him from Teyvat with
Dainsleif
's assistance.
Shortly after Rerir's expulsion, Columbina's strength began to rapidly fade, though she hoped she would last long enough to partake in the Moon-Prayer Night festival at which time the kuuvahki would be at its strongest, so she could return to the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. However, her form began to quickly fade due to Teyvat's rejection, and after an initial visit by the Traveler, was barely able to maintain herself. Believing that her time was up, she decided to take a final stroll across the land before running into the Traveler and passing through them. The Traveler appeared to sense her, and seeing this, Columbina decided to follow them, though she found herself unable to enter the Hyperborean temple. Returning to Silvermoon Hall to meet her end, she soon found the Traveler calling for her name, allowing her physical form to be restored. Reuniting with them, she decided to give herself the name Columbina Hyposelenia.
She later partook in the Moon-Prayer festival and after spending some time with her friends, she prepared to leave. She was intercepted by Dottore, who then froze time to stop everyone but the Traveler and began overpowering them. Realizing he was drawing his power from her and with no choice, Columbina willingly took herself out of the equation and fell into the moon's reflection, stopping Dottore from fully harnessing his new power and causing him to retreat. The rest of the group swore to stop Dottore and pinpoint her whereabouts.
Inside the moon's reflection, Columbina tried to influence various events that had occurred to no avail. She discovered that she was travelling backwards in time and sought to find a way to inform her friends of what was going on. She eventually achieved this by leaving a message in the Traveler's room after they defeated Rerir, which was later discovered by Nefer and Flins in the present. During her journey, she ran into Rerir, immediately being on guard from him. Rerir apologized for his actions, revealing that Dottore had revived him and that after meeting Dainsleif, he decided to stop running from his fears. He helped remove several obstacles in her path and asked her to give a message to Tholindis. She did so and Tholindis guided her further down the path where she was soon discovered by Sonnet. Sonnet brought her to Canon and Aria, where Columbina told them of the events that would happen in the future. She asked to borrow their powers to use them against Dottore, which they agreed to after seeing her resolve. She also gave them their wish to be granted peace as mortals by bringing them out of Nibelung's prison before "dying" herself, and then, coupled with Sandrone's World-Formula working their computation, she was able to return back to Teyvat at the most anticipated moment where all of the formula's conditional events were successfully met.
Columbina and her friends overpowered Dottore before she defeated him off with a large bleam of energy. Following the battle, she noticed Sandrone lying on the floor and cradled her in her arms. While saddened at the loss of her friend (even trying to use her new powers to revive her to no avail
), she gave words of encouragement to Arlecchino, who appreciated her new growth. Since the implementation of the Palestar Edict proved to be nothing more than a means for Dottore to seize Columbina’s power, the Tsaritsa revoked the warrant on her, letting her go as she pleased. She then had the Traveler meet her on Hiisi Island, having decided to live with the Frostmoon Scions once again. She brought them to the Frost Moon and revealed that their spaceship had been placed there by the
Heavenly Principles
, either because something there was incompatible with Teyvat or there were secrets they wished to keep hidden. As they gazed at the planet, the Traveler asked Columbina a few more questions about their next destination, Snezhnaya.
Columbina then returned to Silvermoon Hall and revived Luonnotar, giving her free will and passing down the memories it had of the Traveler.
Later on, Lauma had a large amount of food delivered to her while having the statue of her adorned with wings. The Frostmoon Scions finished the project and Lauma sent a
Longplume Ibis
to inform her to come, while the Traveler asked her to not use kuuvahki to sense her surroundings. They arrived at the newly refurbished statue, where she opened her eyes to look at it, appreciating the new design before Lauma invited her to a feast, which she accepted.
Later, she took Maalaus to the Frost Moon, who created a painting of the Traveler and Columbina observing Teyvat from the moon. Columbina considered both the Frost Moon and Teyvat her homes and planned to bring more kuuhenki to the moon.
The Traveler later played Moon Chess and a new game with her. She then took an interest in cooking; although a bit inexperienced, she was a quick learner and secretly slipped gifts for her friends and gave the Traveler a
new wind glider
for their companionship.

Etymology
In
commedia dell'arte
, a type of Italian theater,
Columbina
(
Italian
:
Colombina
"Little Dove"
) is a female servant who is often the "only functional intellect on the stage" as she does her best to get the
Innamorati
together.
Kuutar
(meaning "Lady Moon"), is the Finnish goddess of the Moon. She is said to own the gold of the Moon, spinning golden yarns, and weaving clothes out of them. Additionally, she represents the New Moon.
Hyposelenia, her epithet, is derived from the ancient Greek prefix
ῠ̔πο-
(hŭpo-), meaning "beneath" or "under",
σελήνη
(selḗnē), meaning "Moon" as well as being the name of the Greek Moon Goddess, and the suffix
-ῐ́ᾱ
(-ĭ́ā), which the addition of makes a feminine noun. All together they mean "Beneath/Under the Moon".
In addition, one of Columbina's titles (Lord of the Crossroads) is based on another Greek Moon Goddess,
Hecate
, who is associated with crossroads, magic, and witchcraft.
In game, Columbina stated that the name Hyposelenia meant "Born Beneath the Moon", with the "Born..." part of it most likely being an implied additive.

## Profile

Kuutar, the Moon Goddess of Nod-Krai, and formerly The Damselette, Third of the Fatui Harbingers. Still, her friends call her Columbina Hyposelenia, and she prefers it that way.
—Description from the Official Website
The Moon Maiden born in
Nod-Krai
, and also the welkin moon returning home.

Official Introduction
Version 1
Moonlit Dove, Where Lies Your Cove?
Winter nights know no mercy. She left her curtained nest in frost and snow; no one is obliged to restore that which she has lost.
—
Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya
To Columbina, this
planet
was never a kind mother.
It gave her a singular, extraordinary identity when it birthed her — but that seemed to exhaust all the love it could offer. In the days that followed, her mother rejected her outstretched arms with cold silence. Columbina could only close her eyes and grope forward between one wall and another.
The maze that trapped her was a circle, a spiraling tower reaching upward. Columbina softly hummed the melodies in her memory, her only connection to her "mother." Barefoot, she walked through the
Frostmoon Enclave
and
Glupov
, eventually returning to the
Silvermoon Hall
, where her own echoes still lingered on the walls.
But unlike before, she had finally found
a name
of her own with the help of her friends. She now had hope, as well as desires.
She wanted to go home. Now, at the top of the tower, the
moon
that held her answers hovered just within reach.
She stretched out her arms, but it was like the moon falling into water.

Of Silver Skies, Cleansed Feathers, and the Returning New Moon
Huh? What kind of question is that? Of course, I don't like her. She spends her days bumming around and singing, drops in on tea parties whenever she feels like it, and has zero social graces. How has she survived this long anyway, prancing around being all nice and naive!? Hmph. If reality ever hits her hard, I'll be watching. Laughing. Don't count on me to step in.
—From a conversation between
Marionette
and
The Fair Lady
at a tea party
She walked toward death, and death answered her with rebirth. She reached the end, only to find it waiting as her beginning.
The world waited in the hush that preceded creation.
Within that silence, a force stirred — gravity was cast toward her, strong enough to draw a wandering moon back into its swaddling cloths. Before her, the moon's path revealed itself, a road laid out to guide the Moon Goddess home.
She is no longer the moon seen through water, a distant image, endlessly shattered and restored.
Instead, she will sing fate into the darkness, set the tides in motion, and bring freedom and hope to those who dwell beneath the moon.
Columbina opens her eyes, as though only now she is truly born into the world.
Character Stories
Character Details
Day or night?
Why, night, of course. The world of daylight was filled with too many words beyond her understanding. "Kuutar," "The Damselette," "Goddess"... She knew these words to be like mirrors, each reflecting a version of her in this unfamiliar world. But those were versions others wished to see. She did not recognize herself in those reflections, just as she did not recognize the moon hanging in the sky. At least at night, she could clearly feel the pull coming from beyond
the heavens
. It made no sound, yet she understood it all the same — the
Frost Moon
was calling her home.
...
Spring, summer, autumn, or winter?
Winter, without question. After the restlessness of spring, the clamor of summer, and the bounty of autumn, winter's snow quietly laid a finger across the world's lips, as if to say, "Hush. After all that bustle, isn't it time for some quiet?"
And so the world would obediently fall silent. Little animals would retreat to their homes as well. Only in winter would people truly realize how important a warm "home" could be.
As for Columbina? She loved to find a thick, soft pile of snow, before holding her breath and diving straight in. For a
goddess
who wasn't particularly sensitive to cold, being wrapped head to toe in gentle white snow felt simply wonderful. As to whether her colleagues understood this behavior... Well, she concerned herself little with that.
...
Sunny? Rainy? Or perhaps, cloudy?
A rainy morning. When Columbina woke to the soft patter of rain beyond the Silvermoon Hall, a sense of calm would settle over her — the feeling of being sheltered beneath its vaulted roof.
"Shelter." In the rhythm of the rain, the word resonated with renewed strength, echoing the most essential meaning that "home" could hold. Simply existing along the extension of that meaning was enough to fill Columbina with warmth.
Still... nightfall was better when under clear skies.
...
Were her answers to these questions truly accurate? Might they change someday? Columbina turned these thoughts over in her mind.
Probably not. But even if they did, these answers were the truest version of herself in this moment — the truest version of her on this night.
Forests, lakes, and open seas... Which did she love most?
She loved them all, of course.

The first thing she heard was a song.
♪ Sleep, sleep now, my dear little dove ♪...
♪ May flowers and lovely dreams bloom by your window ♪...
...
Whose voice was that? It sounded distant and indistinct, like a lullaby — like the beginning of a story, and just as much its ending...
Then came the sensation of the earth beneath her.
Fine-grained, warm, and gentle, carrying a familiar strength, like a part of her own body, or the amniotic waters that once cradled her before she was born...
And so, she opened her eyes.
Moonlight
drifted through the air, and a few
little ones
caught its fleeting threads and wove them into garments for her.
Something far, far away was calling her — a pull, a connection, like a yet-unbroken umbilical cord between a baby and its mother.
She lifted her eyes. But there was nothing in the sky, only a veil. A lie. For some reason, the first time she saw a sky full of stars, she knew it was false. And whatever it was that connected her to something beyond was cut off by this veil of lies.
Why was she born here? Or rather, why was she born at all? Who was she? And whose voice did that singing belong to?
Questions arose, one after another, from the depths of her mind. Yet the world offered not even an echo in reply. Sometimes, not hearing was scarier than not seeing.
She sensed that these questions might follow her forever.
She closed her eyes. The truth was not to be found beneath a veil of lies.
Character Story 2

When Columbina first came to the
Fatui
, she immediately found them very different from the
Frostmoon Scions
.
No one seemed to make unreasonable demands of her. No one appeared to desire anything from her or revere her — at least, not on the surface.
The concept of "colleagues" was new to her, too. Within it, she sensed something both unfamiliar and deeply appealing: a sense of equality, occasionally mixed with a familiar notion of "trade." She was still learning to navigate this way of life.
The first to approach her was
The Doctor
. Columbina could hear admiration in this colleague's words — not the same as worship; at least, it was based on the premise of equality.
"If you don't mind, could you help me with a small task?" the colleague asked politely.
Help? She had expected this to be something she was obliged to give in return, but here, she actually had a choice. Or perhaps it was this very colleague who was offering her the choice.
Even though The Doctor's words carried a faint echo of something ominous, like distant thunder from a far-off storm, the sense of having a choice was undeniably satisfying.
At first, everything went smoothly. The Doctor's lab was filled with all sorts of strange devices, and Columbina only needed to lightly apply her power here, manipulate that there, and everything would work out.
"Not bad. You did very well."
Columbina liked praise. It was new to her, a novel experience.
But before long, compliments shifted to "Not quite enough," "You can do better," or "You're capable of more..."
Suddenly, Columbina realized that what The Doctor was seeing wasn't her, but some version of what she should be, or the level her powers should reach. It reminded her of the days before she had joined the Fatui...
She felt a pang of sadness. Did anyone else ever feel sad just for being themselves? She didn't know if others felt that, but she did.
It wasn't that she wanted to become what others expected, but that what they saw wasn't her. The real her, like an unwelcome child at a party, was left alone on the sidelines...

...Might as well get something to eat.
When she was feeling down, Columbina always turned to the things she liked doing. Filling her stomach was just one of them, and the easiest to satisfy.
"I—I'm really sorry, Lady Harbinger..." As she was leaving the restaurant, someone called out to her, trembling, and handed her a slip of paper. Columbina couldn't see it, but somehow remembered — this was part of a process that had to happen after eating. Different from how procedures were back with the Frostmoon Scions.
The problem was, she still wasn't used to it. She knew it involved some ritual of "trade," but the few times she had done it, it was always with her two recently acquainted colleagues,
Arlecchino
and
Sandrone
. Or rather, they did it for her.
Columbina didn't know what to do. During that moment, she felt a kind of fear and guilt radiating from the other person, so palpable she could sense it even with her eyes closed. It was her fault, and she felt both sorry and helpless.
"I..." Columbina searched for unfamiliar words.
"Alright, I've got this. You just stay out of it." A familiar voice appeared beside her. It was Sandrone.
"Hmph. You don't even know how to apply for reimbursement, and you dare come out to eat by yourself? Ugh. You know what, I bet you don't even know how to pay either." The tone was impatient, but somehow reassuring.
"The Fatui's financial rules sure are rigid... and complicated," she muttered, quickly filling out the slip and handing it to the trembling employee.
"From now on, put her bill on my tab. It's all the same when it comes to reimbursement anyway," Sandrone said irritably to the employee.
"Sorry..." Columbina murmured to her colleague, with whom she had not been acquainted long. She knew she was being helped.
"Huh? I didn't do this just to hear you apologize." Somehow, the other sounded even more annoyed. Why?
"Hmm? You don't blame me?" Columbina asked.
"Huh? Why? Isn't this just who you are?" came the exasperated reply. "Seriously, though, I don't get how someone like you even survives in this world..."
No "You should be like this," no "You could do better"... only "This is who you are."
Though Sandrone's tone was always impatient, there was a recognition in it — a silent acknowledgment that this was "who she really was." Columbina felt joy at being seen in this way.
"...What are you smirking at?" Sandrone asked.
Columbina shook her head.
"Nothing. Let's go eat together next time, Sandrone," she said. "I like seeing you handle the 'reimbursements' for me."
"...What?" It seemed her dear colleague would need some time to get used to how "she really was" all the same.

True Moon
On nights when the moon was full, Columbina liked to quietly leave the
Silvermoon Hall
and wander near the homes of the little animals. Sometimes it would be a tree hollow, at others a bird's nest. She would stand nearby, listening to the steady rhythm of their breaths while lifting her eyes to the moon above. It was as if, by doing so, she could imagine what nighttime on the moon might be like — quiet and peaceful. She hoped it was.
On nights like these, she pieced together dreams of what a "home" might be.
When she once again returned to
Hiisi Island
, she met a certain contemporary
Moonchanter
. Columbina had spent much time with the
Frostmoon Scions
, yet she had never once seen such beautiful, enormous antlers.
"...Forgive my interruption,"
Canon
, the goddess of the
Frost Moon
, interrupted. "I thought there were quite a few with antlers among the Frostmoon Scions."
Columbina explained that while it had been historically common, few were born with antlers today, and none boasted antlers as splendid as that Moonchanter's.
"The bloodline inevitably grew diluted, I see..." The Frost Moon goddess sighed. "Sorry... Please continue, Columbina Hyposelenia."
After meeting that Moonchanter, Columbina occasionally made surreptitious visits to her home as well. She discovered, however, that the moon's tides seemed to bring some restless pain to said Moonchanter. In those moments, Columbina would try to use her own power to soothe the tides surrounding the woman. Sometimes, she would find herself humming familiar melodies, instinctively — the lullaby that had once comforted her before her birth.
"A lullaby from before your birth?" This time it was
Aria
, goddess of the
Eternal Moon
, who spoke up.
Columbina explained the melodies she had heard before being born.
The three goddesses shook their heads. They had no knowledge of this song.
Columbina continued to tell them stories of the future world: From the flavors of offerings in that world, to the music in its opera houses... From a
friend
with a clockwork mechanism in her back, to a
former angel
who became her friend...
Finally, she spoke of the
Moon-Prayer Night
.
The
three moon
goddesses were deeply moved by the beauty of that evening — perhaps also because Columbina herself could not help but smile as she recounted it.
"In our time, the gods of the heavens rarely had such profound connections with the world below," the goddess of the Frost Moon said.
"Yes. Everyone was nice to me. We even played water-ball games together."
"Unimaginable. It seems our era was far too conservative," the goddess of the Eternal Moon said, clearly astonished.
"We even stuck little stickers on each other's faces."
A few soft gasps of surprise came from the three goddesses.
"We also exchanged food, tasting the candy in each other's hands."
The goddesses fell silent. Columbina felt glances tinted with envy pass between them.
Long moments passed. Then
Sonnet
, goddess of the
Iridescent Moon
, finally breathed a sigh of amazement.
"Wow..."

Home is acceptance.
The
Frost Moon
passed through the
false sky
and remained in
Teyvat
. Columbina Hyposelenia, too, was accepted by this world. Now, it was her turn to try to accept the world in return.
The first thing she did was move aside the massive boulder blocking
Silvermoon Hall
's entrance. It had once been placed there to keep the
Frostmoon Scions
and the
Fatui
from finding her. Now, it no longer seemed necessary.
Her friends in
Nod-Krai
often told her she was welcome to visit their homes. It was only right that she did the same for her friends, and for her "family."
Home is freedom.
There were no longer rules rejecting her existence. No places she was forbidden to go. No one coming to seize her...
She could go wherever she wished, do whatever she pleased.
Of course, learning to deal with others came with its share of stumbles. Once, while strolling through
Nasha Town
, she nearly fell for a malicious scam and was sharply scolded by
Nefer
, who had to save the day. Another time, while wandering freely through Nod-Krai, she accidentally entered a restricted area of
Piramida
and
Illuga
had to politely escort her out...
Well, being scolded, too, was part of life among family. Columbina understood that now.
And of course, so was learning to correct one's mistakes.
Home is...
Hehe, home can be many things, Columbina thought.
It is found in the small details of everyday life — in all the fresh, unfamiliar things she has only just begun to notice and experience.
And it is something that will remain with her, always, throughout her journey in this world.
An Eye Mask Woven of Moonlight
Friendship Lv. 4
•
True Moon
To Columbina, the world's "truth" is often revealed only when she closes her eyes.
After sensing that the sky's
great dome
was nothing but a fabricated lie, she veiled her sight with gauze woven from
moonlight
. It was no inconvenience to her — if anything, the world perceived through kuuvahki felt clearer, more lucid than what the eyes could see. And should curiosity ever get the better of her, she could always steal a glance. After all, her eye mask had fine gaps in it.
The stars may lie, but the tides of gravity do not. People may conceal their expressions, but the emotional currents rising from within their hearts cannot be hidden.
Columbina waited, intending to remove this eye mask that shut out falsehood only after she returned to that true "home."
Yet after becoming the Trilune Goddess and returning to
Teyvat
, she remembered the words
Canon
, the goddess of the
Frost Moon
, once spoke to her. Tentatively, she began opening her eyes in daily life, discovering the world anew.
She discovered that the spring forests held so many colors, and that the winter snow could be so pure and luminous.
That the spray cast up as waves struck the shore was never the same twice, and that the lights of a city at night shimmered like a brilliant, star-filled sky...
She felt fortunate.
Perhaps she should have opened her eyes sooner, she sometimes thought.
But then again... perhaps it was her experiences up to now that had taught her to truly see the world's beauty. If she had opened her eyes earlier, would she have only seen the lies the world hides?
Columbina wasn't sure. Such introspective questions would take time to answer.
In the meantime, she decided to occasionally open her eyes and take in the beauty of this "home."
Why not keep her eyes open all the time, then?
"Hmm... I've just gotten used to it, I suppose. And also," Columbina rubbed her eyes, "keeping them open all the time is tiring. Is this... what they call 'eye strain'?"
Tri-Lunar Convergence
Friendship Lv. 6
•
True Moon
After acquiring tri-lunar authority, Columbina naturally had some questions:
Could this power really do everything? What should she use it for?
She felt that the things she wanted to do were almost all within the reach of the
three moons
' power: Moving freely between the
moon
and
Teyvat
, effortlessly transporting herself and other objects, making flowers bloom more brilliantly, causing fruits to grow fuller...
She even tried to wake
Sandrone
up. But that was one of the few things this trilunar power could not accomplish for her.
As for everything else, she either didn't want to do it, or she felt that she shouldn't.
What she loved was the way this world and her home looked now. Changing it at will just because she could, molding its appearance and rules to suit her own whims... She felt that would be wrong. No matter how good her intentions, doing so would make her no different from
The Doctor
, she felt.
She regarded this world as her "home," not as her possession, and there was a great difference between the two.
So she offered a portion of her power, that it may continue to protect this beautiful home as it always had. The part she kept for herself needed only to allow her to live happily with her friends, as before, and to guard what she cherished when necessary.
In that moment, Columbina suddenly understood the choices her
three
elder
sisters
had made. Rather than seeking to "rule," what they had wanted most was simply to "protect" this world beneath the moon.
Could she say that she was their little sister, after all? The thought brought a sweet sense of harmony.
It felt almost like an inherited trait, or perhaps a "family tendency" moon goddesses had.
A warm feeling rose in Columbina's heart at the thought. She seemed to have again discovered something she had longed for:
The bonds and legacy shared among family.
