# Lisa

## Storyline

Lisa
is a woman who oversees Mondstadt's Library at the
Knights of Favonius Headquarters
. In her youth, she was considered one of the best students of the
Sumeru Akademiya
, but she chose to abandon her prestige to live a carefree life as a librarian after seeing the decay of human life there firsthand.
Profile
Personality
The languid but knowledgeable Librarian of the Knights of Favonius, deemed by Sumeru Akademiya to be their most distinguished graduate in the past two centuries.
—In-game character attributes and profile page text
Lisa is a proficient witch; as such, she enjoys everything related to the elements and items that would help her understand them. Despite her position as Librarian, Lisa takes it somewhat haphazardly as she appears to be relatively bored dealing with normal books, instead preferring those in the restricted section. She still takes the job seriously nonetheless, and will personally go out to collect books that are long overdue, albeit in a way that she can still enjoy the smaller things in life.
Her haphazard, carefree personality comes from her time in Sumeru Akademiya, as while studying there she noticed the types of effects the pursuit of knowledge can have on a person. She studied in the Spantamad school for two years and left Sumeru shortly after graduating,
relegating herself to categorization and management of the library and usually refusing to take any other duties unless absolutely necessary. Despite repeated pleas from
Gafoor
to return to the Akademiya, Lisa refuses to give up her life in Mondstadt.
When Lisa is angered, the surrounding air near her becomes electrified in part due to her Electro Vision. Depending on the severity of what has angered her, she can either be simply irritated or become extremely scary, the latter also causing her to dispense "appropriate" punishment.
Lisa seems to be fond of the
Traveler
, and sometimes acts flirtatious, referring to them as "cutie" or "darling," and admitting that they're special to her.
She loves tea and thinks that afternoon tea is the most important part of her day. She sometimes takes tea with vegetable soup as she once used to enjoy this with
Jean
at the
Good Hunter
restaurant. She also suffers from cucurbitophobia, a fear of pumpkins.

History
Past
Lisa attended Sumeru Akademiya in her youth, studying under the Spantamad school for two years under
Cyrus'
tutelage. She was also acquainted with
Cyno
, considering him her junior. During her time in the Akademiya, her aptitude led her to being considered the best student they had seen in two centuries. At the same time, she saw the decay of humanity in their attempt to obtain knowledge, which deeply disturbed her and she left the Akademiya, returning to Mondstadt. There, she chose to take up the position of librarian at the library. Gafoor would write letters to have Lisa come back, but she refused to do so.
Though Lisa was seemingly carefree, she would change when people began to forget returning the books they borrowed from the library. While she accepted their excuses, she found it exasperating to chase after each person. The only exception was
Fischl
, who Lisa liked as she always returned her books the way they were and on time.
Lisa was also responsible for teaching
Razor
after he was brought to Mondstadt by
Varka
.
She assisted the investigation into the Black Fire Incident by compiling reports for the Knights of Favonius to read through. She also contacted her teacher to request assistance in deciphering the ancient language found on one of the Fatui's files. Due to her request, Cyno traveled to Mondstadt and was able to seal the archon residue
inside
Collei
. Lisa also assisted Collei in writing a letter to
Amber
in which Collei thanked Amber and said she left for
Sumeru
with Cyno.
Present
The Traveler first meets Lisa during the Stormterror crisis; she takes a liking to them and proceeds to call them a "cutie." Due to
Dvalin
's energies running rampant and as a mage, Lisa felt his energies and was displeased, claiming she was one elemental particle away from a full-blown breakout. She pinpointed the energies' origin to the Four Winds' Temples.
The Traveler accompanied Lisa to the Temple of the Lion, where they cleared the monsters and the energy source within.
Lisa was pleased after the Traveler resolved the Stormterror crisis and returned to the library. The Traveler, wanting to learn more about the nations, visited her. She suggested the Mondstadt edition of the
Teyvat Travel Guide
, only to realize the book had not been returned, which quickly led to her noticing other books were also overdue. Deciding the borrowers had taken too long, she decided to recover them herself, doubling the recovery as an outing with the Traveler. Upon reaching the last person,
Donna
, she reveals to her that the book might've been stolen. Lisa reassures her that it isn't her fault and tells the Traveler that all of the books were marked with elemental energy, so the thief could not have gotten far. They eventually discover an
Abyss Mage
had stolen the book, believing it had a secret power hidden in it. The mage refused to return the book, prompting Lisa to electrocute it with a blast of Electro energy. They recover the book, and Lisa allows the Traveler to indefinitely keep it as a "custodian" rather than "borrower." They return to the library, where a new shipment of travel guides had arrived, where she gives one to them. She also gives them some souvenirs for helping her reclaim the book.
Taking note of Jean's work across the city, Lisa and the other Knights of Favonius decided to arrange a party for her. During the party, Lisa told the Traveler that Jean was upstairs, cryptically telling them that they could talk to her without others listening.
During the first
Windblume Festival
, Jean had Lisa accompany her on patrol to handle any drunkards they came across. Lisa would handle them by using her powers to electrocute them, much to her exasperation.
They later concluded the patrol and shared a meal.
After Klee obtained a threatening letter on Dodoco, she and the Traveler went to Lisa for answers. Lisa suggested they use Elemental Sight, revealing additional script which they transcribed. She told them that she had heard of these islands through various books in the library, with many considering it to be a legend. She did not know the location of these islands, telling them to look for someone more knowledgeable on the matter, as she spent her time inside.
Varka had
Mika
return to Mondstadt for the upcoming
Weinlesefest
and had him carry a letter back home. In it, he told Lisa that he believed it was time for Razor to learn his past, having kept a box of his parents' mementos inside the office. The Traveler noticed Lisa's concern, who told them that as his teacher, she felt that he would close himself off to her if he felt upset. As such, she asked the Traveler to do so on her behalf, as he saw them as friends and she believed it was better he learned from them instead. The Traveler brought Razor to the office, where they discovered a Thousand-Wind Wine. Lisa explained its origins and asked Razor to smell it before telling him they had other options to solve the problem.
As he solved the problem with his friends, he came upon a roadblock. Lisa, believing another tutoring session was due, told the Traveler to wait a few days for the final ingredient before easing Razor's heart.
Lisa later returned to Sumeru for an annual reunion with her fellow Darshan. She had wanted to meet with Collei, but was found by Gafoor, who asked her to return, as she could be an exceptional Herbad. Lisa refused, as she did not want to return to the decay she had witnessed in the Akademiya and was content with her duty as a librarian. Garfoor respected her decision and left; seeing the Traveler's curiosity, she explained to them why she had left. She then asked them what they were doing and was surprised to hear they had resolved a major crisis in the nation, pleased at how much they had grown. She would spend a few more days across the rainforest before returning home.
While fish blasting across Mondstadt, Klee came upon non-native fish and reported it to her seniors. To ensure Klee could enjoy some time while the Investigation Team handled the matter, Lisa decided to create the Dodoco's Bomb-Tastic Adventure to keep Klee entertained while the situation was being sorted out. The Traveler ran into both and played the game with Klee.
The situation was later resolved and Lisa explained what had happened after the Investigation Team concluded their finds.
Wishing to increase the public perception of alchemy, Jean asked Lisa to help out on the endeavor. Lisa, having had some knowledge in potions, agreed, but was at a standstill until the Traveler met with her. She asked the Traveler to help out with a newly opened potion shop and gave them basic pointers before leaving them to their own machinations.
As the shop grew, Lisa realized there were other areas to improve business and told them to speak with
Freda
for more information.
When monsters infested by Durin's energies attacked Mondstadt, Lisa was forced to step outside and defend the city from the attackers. She used "gentle" electrocution to kill the monsters, finding it relieving to let loose once in a while by going out.
The Windblume Festival that was held was also a distraction for Albedo to bring forth a new and friendlier
Durin
. As Durin slowly acclimated to the people of Mondstadt, Lisa taught him how to read and write.
Lisa and the others later met
Lohen
in the library, where he gave them letters sent by Varka explaining what was going on in Nod-Krai. She had a stockpile of documents in the archive that needed his review, and she promised to make sure that he handled each one rather than run off. She then suggested they speak to Jean, believing she would be relieved with his return.
The letters were meant to help anchor him to the physical world rather than the Ley Lines, and as such, Lisa wrote a letter to do so.
Alongside many of Mondstadt's civilians, Lisa participated in Nicole and Alice's memory bubble project, though she found the prospect rather boring and wanted to return to her leisure activities.
She later met the Traveler at a temporary bar stand, asking for a drink to revive her energy and mood. The Traveler concocted a Snow-Kissed Dawn for her, which she found too energizing for her. The Traveler later made a new drink after talking to her about various topics, which she found much better, as she was no longer tired nor overstimulated. She decided to name the new drink "Slumbering Rose."

The cover of the book in Lisa's Idle 2 bears resemblance to a real-life book titled
The Golden Key
by Henry van Dyke. The book consists of twelve separate stories, all themed around the concept of “deliverance.”
Meanwhile, the in-game book's text is a fragmented repetition of an unrelated poem excerpt: “If wind had colors in spring, it should be green. Flowers come out in spring. When green wind blew around, all the flowers seemed to be surrounded by green leaves. What a beautiful sight!” The complete poem can be found in elementary-level Chinese resources for English learning.
The real-life book's cover features the initials “MA” (a signature made by the binder of the book, Margaret Armstrong). Similarly, what appears to be the English initials “Z.S” can be found on the in-game book's cover.
The open book in the artwork of Lisa's Character Outfit,
A Sobriquet Under Shade
contains text written in the
Latin-Based Language
: “Stat rosa pristina nomine nomina nuda tenemus. Cui in res pulchras sed nec pulchritudines credit debes in somnio aut veritate vivit videre.” (Translation: “The ancient rose remains only in its name, we hold empty names. He who believes in beautiful things, but does not believe in beauty, must see whether he is living in a dream or in reality.”)
The first sentence of the excerpt quotes the last sentence of a novel,
Il nome della rosa
(The Name of the Rose), by Italian author Umberto Eco. The novel is an investigative mystery, and one of the settings in the story is a library labyrinth. The library of this novel, ultimately, burns down. The rose quote, therefore, connects the impermanence of the rose with the burnt library, to represent the loss of knowledge and essence, leaving only an empty name to hold. So, the notion of holding "empty names" reflects a meditation on how history, wisdom, and truth may survive only as shadows or symbols due to the impermanence of the physical or accessible world.
The second sentence is a reference to a dialogue from Socrates, found within Plato's
Republic
. Socrates utilizes the concept of “beauty” to explain the difference between pseudo-intellectuals (“lovers of sounds and sights” who define knowledge based on what is perceived) and true philosophers (“lovers of wisdom” who recognize that knowledge consists of eternal, unchanging truths, unlike the perceivable reality that is subject to change). Recognizing beautiful things, versus recognizing the Form of Beauty itself.

Etymology
The given name Lisa is derived from the Hebrew name אֱלִישֶׁבַע Elishéva meaning “God’s promise.”
Minci, Lisa's family name, is likely derived from the Ashkenazi Jewish surname
Mintz
. The name is traced to the Yiddish
minz
, meaning “mint,” and may have been a habitational name linked to the German city of Mainz. As Jewish families migrated from German-speaking regions to Italy between the 13th and 17th centuries, variant spellings - such as Minci - emerged. In Chinese, her surname 敏兹 (敏min兹zī) is a common transliteration of Mintz. The character 兹zī is often used in place of a rough "tz" sound, as can be seen with the standard Chinese transliteration of Hertz: 赫兹 (赫hè兹zī).
Her constellation, Tempus Fugit, is Latin for “time flies.”

Lisa, Librarian of the Knights of Favonius, has great wisdom that is unparalleled throughout the city. She always knows what to do with whatever troubles Mondstadt.
But with so many careless readers and overdue books to deal with, Lisa often gets tired and a bit... drowsy. Never disturb her when she's taking her morning, midday, or evening naps. Otherwise you will come to understand why she is an outstanding knight — in an electrifying way...
Character Stories
Character Details
The librarian of the Knights of Favonius. In addition to her elegance and charm, Lisa is also highly educated.
She is said to have been the most talented sorceress to study at the Sumeru Akademiya in the last two centuries.
It is known that Lisa chose to return to
Mondstadt
after two years of advanced study in Sumeru, but the reason for her decision is not. At present, Lisa's job is to oversee the book collection in the library owned by the Knights.
Character Story 1
Friendship Lv. 2
Lisa is tasked with managing and maintaining the vast collection of books in the Knights of Favonius library, as well as ensuring that their potions are always fully stocked.
The only time that the citizens of Mondstadt see Lisa is when lending or returning library books.
In both scenarios, they tend to encounter Lisa sitting at the counter like a languid cat, yawning as she signs books in and out.
At times, her behavior causes people to doubt whether she is really up to the task of being librarian for the illustrious Knights of Favonius.
Nevertheless, it appears that she performs every task flawlessly, as her work consistently stands up to scrutiny.
Character Story 2
Friendship Lv. 3
The fact that
Cyrus
of the Sumeru Akademiya frequently refers to Lisa as their "best student in two hundred years" is all that needs to be said about Lisa's accomplishments in her studies.
From outlandish forbidden knowledge, to the handling of elementally infused flora, to the most efficient methods of distilling wine... where there is a body of knowledge, the chances are that Lisa is acquainted with it.
This, paired with her ability to explain things clearly and concisely, makes Lisa the sensible person to approach for young knights and alchemists with burning questions.
That is, provided they go and see her at an acceptable hour.
To visit during unacceptable hours — such as in the morning while Lisa is sleeping in, or in the afternoon while she's enjoying her tea — can spell disaster for unsuspecting visitors.
Character Story 3
Friendship Lv. 4
When people meet Lisa for the first time, they often find themselves overawed by the famous alumna of the Sumeru Akademiya.
However, though her attitude to life may appear at first to be one of ruthless efficiency, it is in fact better understood as a product of her incorrigibly lazy nature, which drives her to avoid troublesome issues at all costs.
By negotiating with
Kaeya
, she offloaded the job of restocking and dispensing medication to
Huffman
and
Swan
, and had
Flora
start sending
Donna
over to deliver herbs on a daily basis.
The only tasks that Lisa handles personally are the categorization and management of books and other documents.
This is the one area where Lisa feels more comfortable handling the work herself rather than delegating it.
Character Story 4
Friendship Lv. 5
When Lisa first joined the Knights of Favonius, the
Grand Master
sought to appoint her as Captain of the 8th Company.
This was much to the consternation of the company's Field Officer,
Nymph
, who resented the idea of an academic coming in at such a high-ranking position.
At Kaeya's suggestion, a practice combat session was arranged in which Lisa and Nymph would both display their magical proficiency.
Two minutes into the session, Lisa graciously declined the captaincy on the grounds that "Nymph manifestly possesses the requisite ability to fulfill the role."
For the whole year following, the Grand Master received a constant stream of referral letters from Nymph in relation to the captaincy, and the only person ever named in them was Lisa Minci.
Eventually, the Grand Master began turning over the referral letters to Lisa directly, but every time, Lisa would come up with an excuse to decline the offer.
Undoubtedly, the 8th Company would have been far stronger under her lead, but she deemed that such a level of power was not only superfluous to requirements, it would also be risky for her in a way that most people could never appreciate.
To be clear, Lisa is quite confident that she could handle any situation she came up against. However, additional danger means an additional workload — and that is something that she would never be able to accept.
Character Story 5
Friendship Lv. 6
Having personally witnessed raving-mad scholars in the forests of Sumeru and powerful sages sitting underutilized on advisory councils, Lisa realized what uninhibited erudition can really do to a person.
It seemed such a high price to pay... How much did one have to sacrifice to attain the profoundest knowledge of all?
Put off by this realization, Lisa decided to leave Sumeru.
Since then, Lisa has always stopped short of taking things too seriously.
"Before demanding too many miracles from the gods, first consider if you are willing to pay the price they ask."
These words she has spoken to just three people since arriving in Mondstadt. In each case, it was because she knew it was what they needed to hear.
Special Heating Cauldron
Friendship Lv. 4
The Special Heating Cauldron is a purpose-built cauldron made to a design that no one in Mondstadt could understand. It features time-delay heating, semi-automated ingredient insertion, and temperature regulation functions.
Lisa spent an enormous budget and commandeered the alchemy workshop for two full weeks in order to build this extraordinary piece of equipment.
She boasts that the cauldron is capable of completing any precision heating task to perfection with a maximum of just two pulls of a lever.
But her most common application for it is to brew tea and keep it at the optimal temperature while she is managing her books.
After all, a relaxing afternoon tea session is by far the most important part of Lisa's day.
Vision
Friendship Lv. 6
A
Vision
is a sign from the gods: a sign that you are one of those whom they have chosen to change the world.
Or, maybe it's just one of the many hoops that one has to jump through on the path to unearthing the secrets of magecraft.
After all, understanding the elements is essential to the study of magic, and practical experience is a far more useful means of acquiring this understanding than trying to assimilate the knowledge contained in dusty old books.
"Hmm... I suppose I shall need a Vision, then."
And just like that, as that thought popped into her mind, her Vision popped into her hand.
With the aid of her Vision, Lisa acquired the knowledge that she sought. But she also sensed the deep secret hidden in the shadows of that knowledge.
For whatever reason, the gods gave humans the key to changing everything, but they did not explain the cost involved. Lisa grew fearful of the truth.
The Vision that hung from her neck became to her a bottomless pit filled with sweet delights, lingering at the back of her mind.
So, from time to time, when somebody whom she finds interesting comes along, Lisa will pass onto them her understanding on all kinds of matters.
Perhaps she is secretly hoping that by doing so, one day someone will come along who has the ability to understand the ultimate truth behind Visions.
