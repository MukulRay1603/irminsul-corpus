Klee
is a young girl and the daughter of the
Hexenzirkel
mage
Alice
and the “Seeker”
Kleiner
. She is a member of the
Knights of Favonius
and is also known as the
Spark Knight
. She takes after her mother, often in destructive ways, much to the dismay of the Knights she has been entrusted to.
Profile
Personality
Klee is an energetic and outgoing girl who takes after her mother, Alice. She has a particular talent for creating explosives and is not fazed by the sound of explosions. She adores anything that can blow up like her bombs, such as
Amber
's
Baron Bunny
. She acts like a typical energetic young child who loves playing with her friends. Unfortunately, her definition of playing usually involves explosives; she is particularly fond of "fish blasting", tossing bombs into lakes full of fish.
In part due to her young age, Klee can be very naïve at times and easily confused by topics she doesn't have any context for, such as mistaking Mondstadt's wine industry for a monster,
and wondering why
Diluc
is always grumpy, even going as far as to call him the "weird grown-up". Klee is not fond of seeing people argue, as she believes that they only come when someone was in the wrong and that it doesn't feel right to her.
While she's considered an official member of the Knights of Favonius, Klee's antics often get her into trouble. Like any child, she often tries to flee to avoid being reprimanded, but is eventually caught by
Jean
who sends her to solitary confinement to self-reflect. While she occasionally does try to do so, she almost always quickly forgets and instead uses the time to brainstorm new bombs and explosives.
Klee loves her fellow knights, and is well-loved by them in turn. She is particularly fond of
Kaeya
and
Albedo
. Whenever she heads out of the city to play, she always follows the "survival rules" which Kaeya gave her. Albedo, on the other hand, is an older brother figure who takes care of her. She is also quite fond of Diona, even catching fish to share and offering her Dodoco to pet when Klee unintentionally upsets her. She loves her parents, though she does not see them frequently as they often travel around Teyvat and thus have little time to spend with her.

short female
model. She has pale skin, light red eyes, pale blonde hair with a prominent cowlick, and elf ears.
History
Past
Klee was born to Alice and
Kleiner
, who are both renowned adventurers of Teyvat. She would spend a good amount of time with them until Alice left her in
Mondstadt
, entrusting her to the Knights of Favonius. Klee, understanding that her parents were incredibly busy, would mostly behave on her part. Prior to her departure, Alice taught Klee how to make and maintain
Dodoco
, who saw it as her "oldest and bestest" friend.
Alice also gave Klee a box containing
Barbeloth
's journal, which she kept buried in
Windrise
.
While in the Knights' custody, Klee was given the title of "Spark Knight." She gained an obsession with making Dodoco-themed bombs known as
Jumpy Dumpty
and would frequently use these to blow up various items, particularly fish in an activity called "fish blasting." Before she left on her adventures, Kaeya gave Klee a pamphlet with several rules; to never use the bombs in the city, avoid harming people and not bomb Mondstadt.
As such, Klee would use her bombs outside the city, and would almost always be caught by Jean, who put her in "solitary confinement." Despite this, Klee would use the time to brainstorm more bombs and simply do what she did best after being released, much to Jean's exasperation.
At some point, she acquired a Pyro Vision while working on a bomb by herself.
Nonetheless, Klee was universally beloved by the Knights, befriending just about everyone who interacted with her. When
Rhinedottir
sent Albedo to Mondstadt so he could continue finishing her test, Klee immediately bonded with him. Alice told them to treat each other as if they were family, and as such, she saw him as an older brother.
She had placed a large amount of explosives in order to stop a hilichurl attack, but had placed too much that they changed the entire landscape. This was witnessed by
Six-Fingered José
, and as a result of the battle, the legend of the "Red Knight of the Stormbearer Mountains" was born.
Since Klee did not have basic training skills due to her young age,
Varka
decided to do so after Jean and Albedo brought the matter up to him. Believing she should have some input, Varka asked her too. According to him, she "happily" brought bombs to the training grounds, resulting in an incident in which the three agreed to indefinitely postpone the test until she got older.
Present
Klee was active during the Stormterror events but did not partake in resolving it. The
Traveler
first meets her after the Stormterror incident, where their curiosity is piqued after overhearing several locals talk about who is Mondstadt's strongest. They asked Jean on the matter, but she declined to answer, and Klee bumped into them as they were leaving. Recognizing them as the Honorary Knight, Klee asks them to help her locate her missing bombs. They accompany her to the
Whispering Woods
, discovering a
Pyro Abyss Mage
that Jean warned them about. Klee, not knowing good and bad, calls the mage "Mr. Fluffball" and believes he was roleplaying as a bad guy. Chasing it to its stronghold, Mr. Fluffball tries to use the bomb on them, but it blows up in its face, killing it, leaving Klee anguished as she believed she had broken one of the survival rules; harming someone with her bombs.
Returning to Jean to inform her of the incident, Jean apologizes to Klee for not telling her about the Abyss Order. Since she had eliminated the mage, Jean allows her to attend the next
Ludi Harpastum
, but having sensed that she was planning to go fish blasting, sends her to solitary confinement as punishment, which she resigns herself to.
Shortly after these events, Barbeloth's apprentice,
Mona
, arrived in Mondstadt in order to reclaim her master's journal and challenge its guardian, Klee. When she arrived to face Klee, she realized that Alice had never taught her anything and as such, they could not have their match. Mona then asked Klee about the box, which Klee had buried in
Windrise
. In her haste, she dropped a journal, which Mona read while she and the Traveler went to recover the box. She then gave the box to Mona, who found nothing inside and asked where the original contents were. Klee then told her that the journal she had read was actually her master's. Mona began to panic upon learning this, and Klee, believing she was about to get in trouble, ran away.
During the Traveler's first summer on Teyvat, Klee was given a letter by the Dodo-King which threatened to take Dodoco away if Klee was not worthy of being its companion. Not wanting her friend taken from her, Klee attempted to ask Jean for time off to go to the islands where Dodo-King was, but Jean initially refused out of concern for her safety. She relents when the Traveler and
Barbara
offer to accompany her, and they go to
Venti
, who summons
Dvalin
to bring them to the archipelago.
They ran into Albedo, Diluc, Kaeya and Razor soon afterwards and with their help, discover that Alice had brought them to the islands so Klee could enjoy a good summer with her friends.
Following the removal of the
Sakoku Decree
,
Yae Miko
hosted an
Irodori Festival
in which Albedo was invited, who took Klee with him as a dependent. He told her several stories about the nation, which greatly excited her, though due to his duties on arrival, he could not spend time with her and she remained on
Ritou
. When the Traveler came to participate, Albedo asked if they would be willing to show her around, which they agreed to, much to her excitement. The Traveler cooked her some of the local cuisine and later went to
Naganohara Fireworks
to meet
Yoimiya
. Klee immediately bonded with the young woman due to their love of explosions, though to their dismay, they could not set off explosions during the festival. They then went to
Aratani
to make a story for Dodoco, but she declined, as they were behind schedule. Just as the situation seemed hopeless, Miko arrived and told them that they could do so under the condition that it would be printed in another book at half the intended size. Klee was grateful to Miko for her consideration before learning that the other book in question was Alice's travel guide, making her even happier.
Klee was later put in solitary confinement once again (presumably for fish blasting) before being released for
Weinlesefest
. Since everyone was busy, she was playing by herself when she ran into
Razor
, who was looking for his mother. Klee decided to help him, though they were not successful and she later asked the Traveler to help him before going to play with
Bennett
.
She later ran into
Diona
and told her of the situation, who misunderstood it as Razor wanting to drink wine. The Traveler and Klee clarified the situation for her.
During the
Windblume Festival
, Klee went on patrol with
Amber
,
Eula
and
Noelle
, though it was more of a casual conversation to pass time.
Alice sensed that her lantern at the floating island had been activated and decided to see what was going on; Klee was excited to hear her mother again.
As another summer came in, Klee was reading in solitary confinement when a letter flew in through the window. Sent by Alice, she told her that a friend invited her to visit her; however, this friend was in
Sumeru
, which was too far for the Knights to send Klee on her own, though she was adamant in taking up the invitation. The Traveler and Kaeya agreed to accompany her for the trip. The letter also had "Magic Invites" that would instantly teleport to a spoken location. They decided to start at
Gandharva Ville
, though Klee mispronounced it as Grand Arbor Hill; fortunately, they arrived at their destination without issue. They ran into Amber, Eula and
Collei
, who told them that Tighnari was busy and would not be back for some time. Taking a look at the letter on his behalf, she discerned that the friend was somewhere in the desert. They teleported to the
Desert of Hadramaveth
and located the domain entrance.
The group finds the domain in disrepair, finding its owner,
Idyia
and also
Kokomi
. They fix the domain and Klee decides to take the Traveler with her on the Choo-Choo cart before bidding farewell to the Oceanid and returning to Mondstadt.
Per the usual, upon returning to Mondstadt, Klee resumed fish blasting until she noticed different fish appearing. She informed Kaeya of her discovery, and they learned that fish from Sumeru had inexplicably appeared in
Cider Lake
. Since these foreign fish were not known in the area, they could potentially pose a threat to the nation's economy, so the Knights began to investigate them. Klee was told not to use her explosives to avoid messing with the data; as everyone was also busy, she was left with
Lisa
, who invented the Dodoco's Bomb-Tastic Adventure board game for her. The Traveler arrived and met both in the library when Paimon suggested to read books there, so Klee asked them to play the game with her.
After clearing the game over several days, Lisa told them that the Knights had eliminated the invasive species and found the
merchant
responsible for the incident, issuing her a fine. Lisa then gave Klee a storybook based on the board game, which Klee appreciated.
Klee later ran into the merchant crying on the side of the road and comforted her with plenty of snacks.
Klee accompanied Albedo to
Simulanka
where she played with the locals and Mini Durin for a short time before returning to Mondstadt.
Klee enjoyed spending time with Durin, who was later given a human form by Albedo and began to live in Mondstadt.
They would spend time fish blasting, which Durin initially confused as a way to train himself, resulting in both being sent to solitary confinement whenever they were caught. Mondstadt later hosted a
Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey
, in which Klee attended to play
Windtrace
with
Yaoyao
and
Sayu
. She was also delighted to see Yoimiya again and wanted to go fish blasting with her, but Jean forbade her from doing so as she did not want people to leave with a bad impression of the nation.
Alice later paid a visit to Mondstadt to help Nicole prepare a memory bubble so that the Knights who had left for Nod-Krai could understand what had happened in their absence. Klee visited the Traveler while they were tending to a pop-up bar, serving her a Fairweather Frolic and later a modified version, which she named Dodoco's Duet. As the bubble was being formatted, Alice spent some time with Klee fish blasting at Starfell Lake. She later told her that the bubble was almost ready for her, but the following day, the bubble became tainted by the Abyss. Not wanting her daughter's time to be ruined, Alice provided some distractions for Klee to hold her off for the time being. She arrived just as the memory bubble was purified in an implosion. Jean later learned that the two had gone fish blasting from Amber and sentenced both the mother and daughter to solitary confinement. Inside, Alice gave drawings to Klee explaining that due to a
bad person
, Kleiner could not come, much to her anger, stating that they should "blow him up." Alice then told Klee that due to the Traveler's assistance, he was no longer a threat, prompting Klee to proclaim that the Honorary Knight was the best.

"Klee" is German for "clover." Her constellation, Trifolium, is Latin for "clover".
While clover typically has three leaves (the 'tri' in 'Trifolium'), all of the clover motifs seen on Klee's clothes and in her elemental effects have four leaves.
Four-leaf clover

#### =======================
In the streets and back alleys of Mondstadt, it is said that there is a girl in red, who holds an unparalleled treasure within the Knights of Favonius.
No matter where or when this girl appears, it's not long before everything goes up in flames.
What's more, as the aftermath of the explosions fades away, she disappears without a trace — there are few who have ever seen her face.
Amongst the swirling rumors, there are even those that go so far to say that she is the most powerful within the Knights of Favonius.
Though as far as Acting Grand Master Jean is concerned, using this power for such destruction simply means...
Solitary confinement's gonna need some upgrades!
Character Stories
Character Details
When the patrons of
Mondstadt
's taverns are asked about the strongest knight of the
Knights of Favonius
, three names are likely to come up:
The highly reputable Acting Grand Master
Jean
, Cavalry Captain
Kaeya
, and the mysterious tycoon,
Diluc
.
However, some claim to have witnessed, albeit through bleary, drunken eyes, a knight in red, leveling the entire
Stormbearer Mountains
.
If one wants to find this mysterious knight, then they need look no further than the solitary confinement cell inside the Knights of Favonius Headquarters.
If the cell is found vacant... then something explosively unfortunate is likely about to take place...
Klee is well known for the danger she presents. As an official member of the Knights of Favonius, her strength in combat cannot be underestimated.
As a hyperactive child, meanwhile, her destructive capabilities cannot be overstated.
After all, while other Mondstadt children her age are still playing with regular toys, Klee's favorite toys are... live explosives.
Character Story 1
Friendship Lv. 2
Three years ago, Klee was entrusted to the Knights of Favonius by her parents, who were famed adventurers.
This was how the fates of Klee and
"that boy in white"
became intertwined with their "family" in Mondstadt.
Klee is curious about everything in the outside world. She has an innocent passion for explosives, and is incredibly enthusiastic in all that she does.
Under the protection of the Knights of Favonius, she is protected from harm and those of ill intent.
This young girl soon forged an unbreakable bond with everyone in the Knights of Favonius.
Being sent to the solitary confinement room by Jean, helping
Amber
improving
Baron Bunny
, as well as developing new bombs...
These parts of Klee's life are all invaluable treasures to her.
Character Story 2
Friendship Lv. 3
"The apple doesn't fall far from the tree..."
Jean sighs to herself as she sits down at her desk, head in hands, faced once again with a stack of damage reports.
Carts blasted beyond repair, storage shacks burnt down, forest fires that the knights barely managed to contain...
Not to mention the suspiciously high number of deaths from unnatural causes among the fish of
Starfell Lake
.
There's only one potential culprit behind all of this, and she just so happens to be one of their own...
"Klee!"
Faced with Jean's stern glare, the harmless-looking Klee surrenders all her concealed explosives.
She is then escorted to solitary confinement, despite her protests amidst floods of tears.
This occurs about once or twice a week at the Knights of Favonius.
Character Story 3
Friendship Lv. 4
Of course, Klee always tries to help clean up the messes she's made.
However, sometimes her reckless personality makes things worse still.
For example, she once wanted to apologize to everyone by making some BBQ fish, but ended up blowing the stove sky-high.
Then there was the time she got into a fluster and tried to put the fire out... with wind — which obviously resulted in the whole plain going up in flames.
Klee is not a bad kid by nature, it's just that her curious and playful nature always results in her failing to think before she acts.
And every time she gets into trouble, she guiltily tries to make up for it.
Though once Jean lets Klee out of solitary confinement, there's usually no more than a day or two before the sound of explosions return to Mondstadt.
The "Spark Knight" always manages to live up to her title in a range of unexpected ways.
Character Story 4
Friendship Lv. 5
•
True Treasure
Though Klee always ends up causing trouble for the Knights of Favonius and Mondstadt, she isn't just some rookie who drags the team down.
On the contrary, her skills with explosives can be quite handy if applied correctly.
Once, Jean was able to defeat a horde of invading hilichurls utilizing Klee's special talents alone.
However, Klee also forever changed the face of Stormbearer Mountains in that same operation due to recklessly placing too many charges...
Needless to say, everyone was more than a little shocked, but at least no one from the Knights of Favonius was injured.
Since then, the legend of the "Red Knight of the Stormbearer Mountains" began to circulate around Mondstadt.
Rumor has it that she is cloaked in red, and clutches the greatest treasure the Knights of Favonius have.
However, only Klee knows what this "treasure" truly is.
Character Story 5
Friendship Lv. 6
•
True Treasure
To Klee, the Knights of Favonius are family, and she can't imagine life without them. Her biggest role model, however, is still her birth mother — the avid adventurer,
Alice
.
A highly renowned adventurer and author of the popular
Teyvat Travel Guide
, there is nothing this incredible mother cannot do — as far as Klee is concerned, anyway.
Whether it's the way she used to guide Klee step-by-step through the techniques for adding gunpowder and selecting fuses, or the encouragement she'd give Klee to express her creativity through ever-bolder and more beautiful fireworks...
Or that time she taught her how to identify
Starsnatch Cliff
's load-bearing points, then place explosive charges accordingly to bring the whole thing down...
Or that time they stuck their tongues out at each other awkwardly while being told off by
Huffman
of the Knights of Favonius...
Klee has many treasured memories of her mother.
This was the way things were until her parents left to travel to somewhere dangerous and far away. They entrusted Klee to the care of Albedo and the Knights of Favonius.
Though still small, Klee knew she would one day become just like her mother.
One day, her explosives would become something that her mother would be proud of.
"Dodoco"
Friendship Lv. 4
Dodoco
is Klee's first friend, and one of her best.
A very long time ago, her mother made this doll for her by hand.
Alice affixed it to Klee's four-leaf clover, and hung on her big backpack.
From that day forth, Klee and Dodoco became inseparable.
Her mother said that this would ensure that Klee would never be lonely and that she would have a friend to confide in even when she was alone.
They would also protect each other in times of danger, just like Amber and Baron Bunny.
This, however, caused the Knights no need of worry — considering the other things Alice and her daughter had invented, perhaps this, too, might explode at any time...
"Dodoco" is a name Klee picked herself.
When asked what this name meant, she flushed red for a good long while before replying: "It means... Klee's best friend!"
Vision
Friendship Lv. 6
Asking Klee how she obtained a
Vision
will probably get you nowhere — she doesn't know, either.
A natural-born pyromaniac, Klee had already been acknowledged by a Vision from a very young age.
According to Klee herself, she had been creating a bomb that was "even bigger than super big!"
This was her very first solo project, but the results were far from expected—
Well, she "only" blew her small workshop sky high, leaving a mountain of smoking ash.
But rather than being disappointed, Klee was pleasantly surprised to see a flaming Vision emerge from those ashes.
How did Klee receive this divine acknowledgment?
Was it her talent and passion for explosives, the individualistic nature she got from her mother, or her shining innocence?
Regardless, Klee had already obtained such a coveted item at a tender, carefree age.
Regardless of what she faces as she grows up, she will never give this "present" up.
