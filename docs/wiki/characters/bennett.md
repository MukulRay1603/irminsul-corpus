# Bennett

## Storyline

Bennett
is a young man living in
Mondstadt
who is part of the
Adventurers' Guild
and is the leader and sole member of
Benny's Adventure Team
.
Profile
Personality
A righteous and good-natured adventurer from Mondstadt who's unfortunately extremely unlucky.
—In-game character attributes and profile page text
Bennett has had more than his fair share of hardships thanks to his extraordinarily bad luck, yet he continues to be a strong and hardworking member of the
Adventurers' Guild
. He is optimistic, well-behaved, and full of passion for adventuring. Despite the long and excruciating stretches of bad luck he encounters, he is also inventive, having created different solutions for the problems he faces while adventuring.
Bennett believes his
Vision
is a sign from the gods: a sign that they have not given up on him, just like how the old and or retired veteran adventurers from the Guild — whom he affectionately refers to as "dads" — have not given up on him either.
Appearance
See also:
Fortune's Favor
and
Adventures in Blazing Hue
Bennett uses the
medium male
model. He has pale ash-gray hair, light tan skin, and bright green eyes. It seems that he keeps getting scarred from his various misadventures and categorically terrible luck. He has a prominent scar on his left shoulder.
History
Past
Bennett was born to two parents;
Macani
and
Aroni
, who belonged to the
Collective of Plenty
and
Masters of the Night-Wind
tribes respectively. Macani was the leader of the
Tecpatl's Flintblades Adventure Team
, which consisted of him, Aroni,
Anja
and
Chaco
. After he was born, the two decided to settle down, but Anja convinced the team to go on one final adventure before they did so. They decided to venture to
Tenochtzitoc
, which would end up being their doom, as they were ambushed and overpowered by the
Abyss
. The group prayed for Bennett's safety, sacrificing their form and memories, transforming them into
Asha
. Bennett miraculously survived and was later discovered by an elderly adventurer from the Adventurers' Guild in the
Mare Jivari
.
Viewing him as his treasure, the adventurer decided to return to Mondstadt with him in tow, but died of his injuries before he could tell his story. Other old adventurers from the Guild decided to raise him and were the ones to name him.
This act resulted in him leaving Natlan's borders, and because he was disconnected from Natlan's
Ley Lines
, he became susceptible to Abyssal influence as he was outside the scope of the
Wayob
's protection. This influence manifested as inexplicably bad luck. As he grew older, he viewed the retired adventurers of the guild as his fathers and eventually led his own team,
Benny's Adventure Team.
Unfortunately, his bad luck caused accidents and its members eventually dropped out, leaving him alone. To spare him from the grief of disbanding his own team,
Katheryne
allowed the team's registration to remain active.
During his travels, Bennett would sustain numerous injuries attributed to his bad luck, though he would push through them. These injuries resulted in him getting valuable first-aid experience, with more severe injuries being treated by
Barbara
. He would become acquainted with
Razor
,
Klee
and
Fischl
, befriending them. He eventually obtained a
Pyro
Vision
after pushing through a particularly dangerous incident that could've resulted in his death.
Present
The Traveler first meets Bennett during their first
Windblume Festival
in Mondstadt. He was with Barbara, who had received a letter and mistakenly thought that Jean had been buried. Realizing where the location was, he ran off to the
Thousand Winds Temple
, only to find Razor stuck in a trap. He tried to free him, only to be caught too and had to wait for rescue, which came with the Traveler's arrival. During the wait, he also finished his poem and gave it to Venti. After being freed, Bennett departed with Razor, who had some interesting spots to show him.
Bennett later went to
Dragonspine
to help out rookie adventurers, but failed to arrive on time; he had gotten caught in a trap and was captured by the
Fatui
, resulting in his assigned rookie,
Gerald
, being assigned to someone else. Learning why they had come, they returned to Albedo's camp, where they were met by Albedo and Eula. Amber later arrived, having been tasked by Cyrus to look for him as he had still failed to return.
Bennett decided to assist in the investigation, during which an avalanche nearly buried him were it not for Albedo's intervention.
During the Traveler's first
Weinlesefest
, Bennett learned of Razor's dilemma regarding wine-brewing. He decided to give him something to help, during which he somehow faceplanted on a
Small Lamp Grass
; miraculously, the plant was unharmed, which prompted him to gift it to Razor for his wine. Klee then asked if he would like to be part of her mother's family, which he refused, citing his positions. Klee was dismayed but nonetheless respected his decision, so she gave him a Jumpty Dumpty that would not explode, claiming that it would be happy to make a new friend. Bennett accepted her gift. Later, he grabbed a bottle from his fathers and showed it to Razor, surprised that he had not broken it on the way.
When Durin's monsters invaded Mondstadt, Bennett helped defend the city from the attacking monsters.
After the end of the
Night Warden Wars
in Natlan, Bennett was given an invitation to the
Easybreeze Holiday Resort
by
Cyrus
, which had been sent to the Adventurers' Guild branches across Teyvat, and went along with Venti to explore Natlan. When he arrived, Bennett was surprised to discover his normally bad luck was replaced with good luck, which he wrote down over the next several days. He also met
Mualani
at her family's shop before arriving at the resort. Bennett led an improvised group consisting of himself, the Traveler, Venti,
Chasca
and Mualani, partaking in the resort's activities. When they tried to claim their rewards, the representative,
Ukucha
, discovered the pictures were blurred. They then noticed their Asha companion was missing and began searching for it, discovering a rift. They entered the rift, finding themselves in Mare Jivari. Bennett was separated from the rest of the group and began suffocating, but was saved in time by group with Venti's guidance. He reunited with his parents, who were pleased to see how he had grown and were able to rest in peace. Bennett was relieved to learn about his past and found closure in it before returning to Mondstadt.
He found that his bad luck returned after he left Natlan's borders, though he largely brushed it off as he was used to that.
Bennett began planning a return trip to the resort for his dads and friends as soon as he got home, and working on lucrative commissions to pay for it. After several months, he had saved up enough by his birthday and confirmed all the details for the trip.
Bennett participated in Alice and Nicole's experiment with a memory bubble, roleplaying as a Fatuus with the rest of the Adventurers' Guild. When he and Cyrus confronted the group after the auto-load costume bug was fixed, he was given a gun, but it malfunctioned, forcing him to the sidelines. He then gave the group the access crest.

Etymology
Bennett's name is derived from the Latin word Benedictus, meaning "blessed."
It may also be a reference to
Bennett's fracture
, a fracture of the carpometacarpal joint caused by hyperextension of the thumb that results in reduced strength in the corresponding hand even with surgical repair. This aspect is made more likely by the presence of the thumbs up gesture in his abilities and theming.
His constellation,
Rota
Calamitas
, is Latin for "Wheel of Misfortune."

## Profile

One of the few young adventurers of the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild, he is always plagued with inexplicable bad luck.
He is the only active member of his own adventure group, known as "Benny's Adventure Team," after all the other members decided to "take leave" following a series of unfortunate incidents. As a result, the team is currently on the verge of being dissolved.
Being unable to break the poor boy's heart, Katheryne of the Adventurers' Guild has kept "Benny's Adventure Team" on the books, whilst also hiding from him the fact that all the other members have long since officially left the team.
—Description from the Official Website

Being Unlucky Brings Luck to Others
#### ====================
Bennett is a kind, passionate boy from Mondstadt, who dreams of becoming a great adventurer one day.
But, his bad luck always seems to get in the way...
Inexplicable landslides, running into overpowering enemies, never actually finding any treasure...
But Bennett doesn't let his misfortune get him down. He continues to adventure with as much enthusiasm as he did on his very first outing, bettering himself despite his relentless bad luck.
Fischl
's raven
Oz
compliments Bennett on his robust nature, whilst the boy's combat style, which shows a complete disregard for his own safety, has always fascinated
Varka
. Even
Barbara
, who comes across injuries on a regular basis, is surprised by how often he comes in injured or needing another dislocation popped back into place.
Bennett simply treats his own bad luck as just another stepping stone on his way to becoming a grand adventurer.
Bennett knows in his heart that this is the right attitude for the great adventurers of the future.
Character Stories
Character Details
Benny's Adventure Team
is an exception to the norm in the
Adventurers' Guild
,
Mondstadt
Branch.
Initially, the team mechanism requiring at least 3 or 4 regulars in each team was set up to promote mutual support and prevent accidents.
As for Benny's Adventure Team, however, its only regular member has been Bennett himself.
The lack of team members essentially has everything to do with Bennett's infamous bad luck.
However, being the passionate adventurer he is, Bennett never feels lonely.
Well, maybe once or twice, sometimes...
Character Story 1
Friendship Lv. 2
The adventurer
Jack
was the first victim of Benny's Adventure Team.
Just a few feet away from the treasure, an extremely rare
Geo
Turbulence occurred, tearing the ground apart and opening a bottomless chasm right before everyone's eyes.
"So close, yet so far... like love."
Jack only suffered minor injuries, but he was never the same as an adventurer thereafter. The event seemed to have left him with a broken heart.
Another member,
Royce
, heard someone shout "Heyo!" before losing consciousness in a huge explosion. The culprit,
Klee
, was sent to the confinement room for seven days for mistaking the cave the adventurers were still exploring in for a giant rabbit hole and blowing it up.
Heckler suffered from a severe bout of diarrhea for a week after joining the team. Although it turned out to be food poisoning, he insisted it was because of Bennett's bad luck.
"They'll be back! They're just... on leave. Please don't invalidate my team's registration with the Guild..."
Seeing Bennett beg and plead before her eyes,
Katheryne
sighed and decided to spare him the truth that his "teammates" had long jumped ship.
Character Story 2
Friendship Lv. 3
There was once a predicament of incomparable despair.
A predicament that had all but swallowed an old adventurer.
The scorching flames scalded his skin, the deafening thunder almost ruptured his eardrums, and the hollering winds threatened to rip his soul from him.
At the end of his hellish journey, what awaited him was... a baby.
The old adventurer thought he was the first living thing to pass through this arduous ordeal. But how could he explain that which lay before him?
In that moment, it hit him, and he believed it to be true.
"This baby must be... a child abandoned by the world itself."
Though there were no mighty artifacts, jewelry, or gold, the old man's face did not seem disappointed in the slightest, for he regarded this baby, whose life hung by a mere thread, to be his treasure.
"This child is what I was meant to find on this journey," he thought as he took the baby into his arms.
Even if what he was about to do opposed the will of the world itself, he was unafraid.
Character Story 3
Friendship Lv. 4
The old adventurer died from severe injuries before being able to tell this story, leaving only the child he had saved.
On his deathbed, he only managed four final words: "will," "adventure," and "final treasure."
Several single, childless, old adventurers in the Adventurers' Guild in Mondstadt raised the child as their own and named him Bennett.
Being raised by the Guild, Bennett referred to everyone as "Dad."
"Dad, your dentures fell into the bowl!" "Dad, you should stop wearing that old leather armor. Where are the undergarments I bought you?" "Dad, it's raining, better keep your distance from me so you don't get struck by lightning!"
Taking care of all his aging dads who have no family has taken up most of his time outside of adventuring.
"The dads certainly have found a treasure, haha!"
Cyrus
, the current Guild Master, laughed, giving Bennett a pat on the back.
Though he is unlucky himself, he can at least try to bring luck to those he cares about. At least, that's how Bennett feels.
Bennett the Unlucky still strives to find his own lucky treasure through his series of unfortunate events.
Character Story 4
Friendship Lv. 5
Oz
the Raven,
Fischl
's familiar, said Bennett is "the most tenacious young man in the world."
Indeed, Bennett's share of bad luck throughout his life can be seen from his scars, old and new.
Attacked by monsters, buried in collapsed ruins, falling from cliffs... A wealth of "unfortunate events" has taught Bennett how to deal with more of them in the future.
Barbara
, the Deaconess at the
Church of Favonius
, was once surprised by how skilled Bennett is at treating dislocated joints.
Another gift from his ordeals is his jaw-dropping talent for combat.
"Look at his moves... Doesn't that hurt?" Grand Master
Varka
of the
Knights
was extremely intrigued after seeing Bennett in combat.
It's not that Bennett doesn't feel pain — he has simply gotten used to it. He adapted to the pain as others would to pungent smells or blinding light.
Therefore, the way Bennett dives into battles head-on without reservation has become a signature of his.
Character Story 5
Friendship Lv. 6
What does death mean, exactly? Always walking the thin line between life and death, Bennett can't help but wonder.
Bennett knows that the old adventurer who saved him has left many praiseworthy stories and legends after his death.
He knows that adventurers will not have weeping at their funerals, only toasts made in their names by old comrades.
He knows, to the adventurers of Mondstadt, a "good death" comes at the end of a life given to the pursuit of treasure and the secrets of the world. Once over, their souls are brought back to their homeland by
Barbatos
.
He once feared death.
However, after pondering on this matter some more, he now feels death may be something fortunate for an adventurer...
Not that fortune has anything to do with him.
"Onto the next treasure!" And with that, Bennett puts this thinking business behind him.
Adhesive Bandage
Friendship Lv. 4
Whenever he suffers severe injuries such as fractures or excessive loss of blood, Bennett will go to the
Cathedral
to seek treatment from Deaconess Barbara.
"Sorry, it's me again..." Bennett will say, scratching the back of his head.
Barbara, however, will just shake her head, and with a "let's deal with those scratches as well," she will toss him some adhesive bandages.
Such displays of concern may be inadvertent, but they have a special place in Bennett's heart. He sticks those bandages on his wounds with the care of one pinning a medal to their chest, before thanking her wholeheartedly.
Bennett keeps many such bandages in his utility pouch when he goes on adventures. Ones he gets from his dads, from Barbara, from Katheryne... and from adventurers he sometimes meets by chance after getting injured.
These tiny tokens of goodwill form a tangible collection of fair fortune unique to Bennett.
"Well, since everyone's by my side, I guess I'm not that unlucky after all."
Vision
Friendship Lv. 6
Bennett's passion for adventure is unstoppable.
He wants to constantly explore, challenge, sweat it out, and eventually grow old on the adventurer's path.
But there was once when he met with a true crisis.
Just as that old adventurer had so long ago, Bennett was in a predicament that would cause any other person to despair.
"Losing this much blood... Not good."
But Bennett did not stop. "There's no reason to turn back," he thought.
Somehow, he just knew that all the agonizing misfortune he had suffered up till this point was for the sake of overcoming this precarious position.
But at the end of his hellish journey, what he found at the end was — nothing at all.
"Ha, returning empty-handed is... part of an adventure. I... don't mind..."
At last, his tightly-wound emotions came loose, allowing his many injuries to rob him of his consciousness, and he collapsed.
When he reawakened, he found that his wounds had been cauterized by a
mysterious flame
, stopping both the bleeding and the pain.
A
jewel
pulsed warmly in the palm of his hand, its rhythm in time with the heartbeat in his chest — the beating heart of an adventurer.
This was not the mercy of the world, or that fate took pity on him. Rather, this was his "final reward," one befitting a person with Bennett's burning passion.
