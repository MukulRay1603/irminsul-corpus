# Tartaglia

## Storyline

Tartaglia
, also known as
Childe
, is the Eleventh of the
Eleven Fatui Harbingers
. He is their youngest and weakest member who craves worthy battles.
Profile
Personality
Tartaglia describes himself as "kind of a bad guy", living for the thrill of a fight and causing chaos. As a child, Tartaglia was frightened and hesitant, but he secretly desired to become an adventurer like the ones his father told him about as they fished together. After falling into The Abyss and meeting and training with
Skirk
, Tartaglia's personality drastically changed. Although he appears to relatively friendly on the surface, Tartaglia is a bloodthirsty warrior. The Abyss and his old master, Skirk, made him self-confident, solipsistic, and brash, thriving on the sensation of being alive. A warrior at heart, he constantly seeks ways to grow stronger, regardless of how he obtains said power. Tartaglia considers those who wish to become strong like him to be friends and takes pleasure in battling them on the battlefield. He lusts for combat and grows excited by fighting strong opponents, even if it could mean dying in the process. He uses bows because he is weakest with them, making his victories with them all the more exhilarating.
Because of his pride, Tartaglia is exceptionally reliable at accomplishing the tasks given to him, no matter how difficult they may be or if they go against his values. This, combined with his straightforward nature, makes him rather easy to manipulate. Despite disliking how his plan in Liyue Harbor could potentially harm innocents, he puts it into motion to fulfill his mission in Liyue — only to learn he was meant to fail as part of an even larger plan he was not privy to, much to his consternation.
Amongst the rest of the Harbingers, Tartaglia is considered an oddball and has a life closest to that of a normal person, according to
Sandrone
.
While most of his fellow Harbingers prefer clandestine operations and staying behind the scenes, Tartaglia favors being front and center. He is a public figure known for attending social gatherings and even participates as an actor in plays and theater. As a result, Childe's coworkers are wary of him, while he holds them in disdain for their schemes and "intangible" methods. While he is easily capable of scheming, he only resorts to such approaches when necessary due to his straightforward nature. He also appears to treat his subordinates less harshly compared to some of his colleagues.
Tartaglia dedicates himself to those he cares about wholeheartedly. He is unquestionably loyal to the Tsaritsa and holds her in high esteem, seeing himself as her weapon of war.
He also cares deeply for his family; he sends money, gifts, and letters home often. Tartaglia is exceptionally proud of his three younger siblings and dotes on them frequently, especially his youngest brother Teucer — he is willing to lie and even put himself in mortal danger, so as long as it makes him happy. Although he was once on good terms with Zhongli (even allowing the former
Archon
to use him as financial support), he seems to have taken his deception personally, stating he will only forgive him if he agrees to trial by combat.
Zhongli appears to be unaware of Tartaglia's change in opinion, being under the impression he will still meet him for drinks to catch up after the recent Liyue events.

History
Pre-Fatui Life
Tartaglia is the oldest child in his family, with his younger siblings
Tonia
,
Anthon
and
Teucer
being born sometime after him. They lived in
Morepesok
, a coastal village in
Snezhnaya
, where he was known as Ajax. He would frequently spend time with his father on ice fishing, where he reveled in the tales that his father spoke of.
Desiring to become an adventurer of his own, Tartaglia snuck out when he was fourteen and quickly got lost in Snezhnaya's forests, chased by bears and wolves. He eventually lost his footing and fell into a chasm which led him to the
Abyss
. There, he encountered a mysterious woman who introduced herself as Skirk. Skirk decided to take him in as a disciple much like
Surtalogi
had done for her, training him to adapt to the nature of the Abyss. She decided to keep a fair distance away from his growth, feeling that he could only strive to be better if she did not actively influence him and eventually returned him to Teyvat.
Due to how time flowed in the Abyss, Tartaglia had felt that three months had passed, when on Teyvat, only three days had passed. He returned to his parents, and the time in the Abyss changed him to become a source of strife in the once-quiet village.
At some point, Tartaglia acquired a
Hydro
Vision
.
Joining the Fatui
Believing that his son's temperament had gone too far, Tartaglia's father handed him to the
Fatui
for conscription, hoping that their military discipline could control him. Instead, he saw trained and well-armed Fatuus being decimated by a seemingly young child. This caught the attention of the Fifth Harbinger,
Pulcinella
, who wondered how he became an eye in a vortex of discord. Pulcinella brought Tartaglia into the Fatui to mete out punishment. Tartaglia reveled in the carnage and for his efforts, was eventually chosen by the
Tsaritsa
to become a Fatui Harbinger.
He was given a
Delusion
which
Pierro
personally pinned on him, and at that moment, Tartaglia swore an oath to the Tsaritsa, realizing that she too was also a true warrior.
Tartaglia would be sent to Liyue to help the local
Northland Bank
branch there collect debts, including one from the owner of the
Yanshang Teahouse
and was assisted by
Javert
in this task. After incapacitating the occupants, he discovered a
Sigil of Permission
on the owner, expressing slight disappointment in Javert for failing to notice it. He took it for himself while exposing the owner's fraud to the authorities.
To ease his family, he told his youngest brother Teucer that he was going to Liyue to help sell toys there.
Present
The Traveler first meets Tartaglia after fleeing from the
Millelith
when they were considered a suspect in Rex Lapis' death during the Rite of Descension. Tartaglia stopped the Millelith from apprehending them and introduced himself as Childe, asking if they knew Signora. Due to their experiences with her, the Traveler became suspicious of him, though he told them he also did not like her and was there to help them. Bringing them to the Northland Bank branch in the harbor, he gave them the Sigil of Permission he had taken previously and told them to seek the adepti, explaining that it would protect them from harm.
After finding the adepti, the Traveler returned to Childe, who learned from them that they sought the Seven, though they refused to tell him more about it. Nonetheless, he told them that he knew someone that could break the stalemate.
Shortly afterwards, he brought them to
Zhongli
, who agreed to let them see the Exuvia under the pretense of performing last rites. Childe gave Zhongli a large sum of Mora to acquire the items, and later made a contract with
Baizhu
to supply coconut milk to the pharmacy for
Qiqi
in return for the Everlasting Incense.
In reality, Childe began to prepare to seize the Geo Gnosis from the Exuvia, discovering that it was being held at the Golden House. He quickly overpowered the guards there and soon ran into the Traveler, who realized he had deceived them. Believing that they had arrived first and took the Gnosis, Childe fought them, even making use of his Electro Delusion and the
Foul Legacy Transformation
, but the Traveler was able to hold their ground and defeat him. Returning to normal and exhausted from the technique, he realized that the Traveler did not have the Gnosis and that the Archon was still alive. Though regretful to use a despicable method, Childe revealed that the Fatui had replicated the Sigil of Permission and used a large amount of them in order to awaken
Osial
, believing that Morax would have no choice but to intervene.
While the Traveler caught up with the Millelith and helped them fend off the Fatui from destroying the
Guizhong Ballistae
, Childe met with Signora and Zhongli at the Northland Bank. He was upset to learn that he was made a pawn by Signora, and his actions would lead to him being condemned by the people of Liyue.
Due to his status as a Harbinger, the Northland Bank could not hand him over to the authorities, and as such,
Ningguang
ordered them to pay a hefty fine for the chaos he had caused.
Likewise, many of the Fatui were no longer welcome in Liyue, including the Ninth Company, which were stuck in
The Chasm
to stabilize the region. The members of the Ninth Division would not learn of these events until the Traveler met with
Anton
and brought him and his group a letter revealing what had happened.
Later, as the Traveler explored
Lingju Pass
, they came upon a Millelith named
Daipai
who warned them of the numerous Ruin Guards in the area. While clearing the automatons, they found Teucer, who told them he was looking for his brother and handed them a large bag of Mora. Recognizing the bank on the bag, they went to the Northland Bank, discovering that Teucer was Childe's brother. Childe was likewise surprised to find that Teucer had gone from Snezhnaya to look for him, and he was the only one in his family who did not know his true affiliation with the Fatui. Childe asked the Traveler to help babysit him while he was handling official business, but Teucer quickly grew weary and wanted to see his brother, resulting in numerous awkward moments in the process.
In order to make his brother satisfied, Childe asked the Traveler to accompany him to the ruins and destroyed the machinery creating the automatons while letting Teucer take a "tour" of the dangerous facility. Unable to handle the automatons under the current circumstances, Childe was forced to use his Foul Legacy form to end the battle, but became heavily injured due to not having sufficiently recovered during his bout with the Traveler. Before he left, he gave the Traveler a Mr. Cyclops figurine, which he had originally wanted to give to him when he returned from Liyue. Though unable to see his younger brother off, he had decided to not let him see him injured or it would have left a bad memory. Childe thanked them for their assistance and hoped to work with them in the future.
Childe later attended Signora's funeral in Snezhnaya alongside his other colleagues, where an argument quickly arose. He was unamused by their bickering, stating that it wasn't a good time to start fighting.
Since
Scaramouche
had defected after acquiring the Electro Gnosis, Childe went to
Inazuma
in order to locate the rogue Harbinger. Instead, he found himself in a Domain with powerful enemies and was fighting them when the Traveler,
Xinyan
,
Kujou Sara
and
Shiki Taishou
discovered him. Fearing that Xinyan would attack him if she learned he was responsible for the events in Liyue, Paimon claimed that he was an adventurer from Mondstadt who sought the thrill of battle. Once the truth behind the Domain was discovered, Childe revealed to the Traveler that he was looking for Scaramouche. He was unable to find him and returned to Snezhnaya to await further orders.
Tartaglia was sent to
Fontaine
in order to help out the local Northland Bank branch there with debts. He was sent to collect a debt from
Truand
, running into the Traveler and Paimon when he was about to ask Truand about the owed money. Having not caught up with them for a while, he attempted to talk with them, only for Truand to become angered at his lack of respect and attacked him with his men. Childe easily overpowered them as Truand realized who he actually was; during the fight, his Vision suddenly flickered, though he still overpowered them nonetheless. He then told the Traveler that recently, he had been in a bad mood and felt a restless power stirring inside him. Before he left to spar with
Clorinde
, Childe gave the Traveler his
Vision
, not wanting it to interfere in his duel with her.
In the ensuing duel, Childe felt that Clorinde had been holding back and was disappointed. Nonetheless, he would end up going on trial over the serial disappearances case; while he found the accusation absurd, he nonetheless went to court to appeal. He was unsuccessful in his defense until
Navia
and the
Traveler
arrived with evidence revealing the
true suspect
, but despite this, the
Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale
still deemed him guilty. Disgusted by the charge and refusing to be taken away, Childe fought the
gardemeks
sent to apprehend him and attempted to use his Foul Legacy form, but was stopped and knocked unconscious by
Neuvillette
, who then had him taken away to the
Fortress of Meropide
.
Arlecchino
, learning of the incident, later spoke with Neuvillette in order to ask for his release, but he refused, citing the court system. Since he did not agree with the Oratrice's decision, but still wanting to appear impartial, he offered an alternative; sending the Traveler to the Meropide, which she accepted. The Traveler, along with Lyney, Lynette and Freminet, went into the prison and discovered that Childe had disappeared from the prison. He was not outside either, causing them to wonder where he was.
In reality, Childe found himself in the
Primordial Sea
and encountered the whale in his dreams, which was actually the
All-Devouring Narwhal
. The two quickly fought each other, with Childe maintaining his Foul Legacy form. This was actually planned by the Oratrice in order to stall it from arriving in Teyvat. As the Fontainian authorities pieced together the prophecy and put Furina on trial in order to get her to spill what she knew, the All-Devouring Narwhal emerged to consume the audience. Childe appeared shortly afterwards, helping Neuvillette distract the beast long enough for the people to escape, though he fell unconscious and into the rift that the narwhal made after it retreated.
Neuvillette and the Traveler would later confront and defeat it, after which Skirk arrived and flung both of them into another rift.
Childe was brought back to Fontaine and was headed back to Snezhnaya when he regained consciousness. Since he knew he had just seen Skirk and having many questions for her, he returned to Fontaine and asked Arlecchino to help him locate her, though they were unable to do so and he concluded that he just needed to get stronger. As Arlecchino took her leave, the Traveler and Paimon stopped her and asked her a few questions about the House of the Hearth. Childe wondered if she would betray them, having heard rumors from Pulcinella. Arlecchino dispelled these as baseless, explaining that she simply let them swirl so she could operate more efficiently. Believing her words, Childe apologized to her for his prejudice before he bid them farewell and returned to Snezhnaya.
When the Tsaritsa heard about Dottore's actions in Nod-Krai, she sent Childe to meet up with Arlecchino and Sandrone to deliver a few messages and pick up the refugees that were being evacuated to Snezhnaya. He only saw the former, and while a bit surprised, Childe informed her that the Tsaritsa ordered the Fatui Internal Affairs division to investigate Dottore's conduct. Suddenly, a Fatuus turned into a monster, and he assisted her in taking down the attackers. He was shocked by the revelation while Arlecchino had anticipated Dottore had prepared it beforehand, before telling her that the Tsaritsa also removed the combat exception for Dottore, meaning that she and Sandrone could properly handle him. He then asked about the Traveler, and upon hearing that they were trapped with Dottore, he impulsively tried to charge in, only for Arlecchino to stop him, as they did not know where they were. He reluctantly agreed; before he left, Arlecchino told him to send a message to Pulcinella for him to warn the other nations of the emerging threat in Nod-Krai and that the Fatui were doing all they could to stop it.
Trivia
Tartaglia comes from a large family, with an ambiguous number of older siblings (most likely two older brothers and at least one older sister)
and three younger siblings: Tonia, Anthon, and
Teucer

Tartaglia's birth name, Ajax, is derived from the Greek mythological figure
Ajax the Great
, a warrior who participated in the Trojan War, known as a resistant and strong lancer.
Teucer
was Ajax the Great's younger half-brother and an archer who fought alongside Ajax.
Tartaglia's weakness with bows and preference for melee weapons may be a reference to the mythological Ajax's strength in spears, while Teucer was much more skilled in archery.
Despite being the first playable

Etymology
In
commedia dell'arte
, an early form of professional theatre originating from Italy,
Tartaglia
is a stock character. Unlike some other stock characters, his role in the story typically varies, but he is distinguished by his farsightedness and stutter.
The alias he gives to the party is translated differently across various language versions. Because Tartaglia is his name, languages that used
commedia
names for the other Harbingers' aliases had to use a different alias for him.
His English alias, "Childe," is a direct translation of his Chinese alias.
Childe
is an archaic English word that refers to the son of a nobleman who has not yet attained knighthood.
In Chinese, he introduces himself as "Childe"
公子
Gōngzǐ
, lit. "young nobleman."
The
commedia
names are always translated into Chinese when used as an alias, so there is no overlap between his alias and his Italian
commedia
name.
In Japanese, he introduces himself as Tartaglia before telling the Traveler to call him "Childe"
公子
Koushi
, lit. "young nobleman."
While his name appears as Tartaglia on his dialogue boxes, all other characters call him "Childe." Like in Chinese, the
commedia
names are always translated into Japanese when used as an alias.
In French, Childe always refers to himself as Tartaglia and never uses his alias
Jeune Sire
, which is a direct translation of the Chinese
公子
.
In German, his alias was translated as
Graf
, which means "earl."
His constellation, Monoceros Caeli, can be interpreted as Latin for "Celestial Narwhal."
The
scientific name
for the narwhal is
Monodon monoceros
.
The Chinese name for his constellation is
鲸天座
, literally meaning "Whale Sky Constellation."
Traditionally in mythology,
monoceros
translates to "unicorn" with the more literal translation as "one-horned."
His birth name, Ajax, comes from the Ancient Greek,
Αἴας
Aías
.

## Profile

Meet Tartaglia — the cunning Snezhnayan whose unpredictable personality keeps people guessing his every move.
Don't be under any illusion as to what he might be thinking or what his intentions are. Just remember this: Behind that innocent, childlike exterior lies a finely honed instrument of war.
—Description from the Official Website
No. 11 of The Harbingers, also known as "Childe." His name is highly feared on the battlefield.
—In-game character attributes and profile page text
Official Introduction
Secrets From the Depths of Winter
One can trust him, but one ought not get too attached. He has unusual tastes when it comes to combat — the encounters he craves the most being those that bring him closest to his own demise.
—The cautious counsel of fellow
Harbinger
,
Pulcinella
He cooks, he cleans... He's the bestest big brother ever!
—The enthusiastic endorsement of younger sister,
Tonia
Warm and friendly one minute, ruthless killer the next... His shifting and unpredictable personality can leave those around him at a loss for words to describe him. But in rare, private moments, he dispenses with titles, casts off his armor and the layers of intrigue that come with it, and show his authentic self.
His name is Tartaglia, and he is devoted to the thrill and the physical feel of battle.
Don't be under any illusion as to what he might be thinking or what his intentions are. Just remember this: Behind that innocent, childlike exterior lies a finely honed instrument of war.
Like a velvet sheath that houses an ostensibly ornamental blade, his unpredictability puts people on edge.
Character Stories
Character Details
Despite holding one of the most senior positions in the
Fatui
as one of the
Eleven Harbingers
, Childe very much looks like the young adult that he still is.
He is a wolf in sheep's clothing, however, for under the cheerful and confident appearance lies a deadly swordsman.
He is the youngest among the Harbingers, but one of the most dangerous among their number.
That said, Childe doesn't seem to fit in well with the others.
As a fighter first and foremost, he is quite at odds with this organization of deceit and treachery.
Character Story 1
Friendship Lv. 2
As the youngest Harbinger in the history of the Fatui, Childe is hardly bound by convention, and plays by his own rules accordingly.
Though this lone wolf is not so well-received by his peers and is also at odds with their methods, his cavalier demeanor hides a responsible, watertight meticulousness.
He is a proud man of his word. Even if the promise he makes is absurd, he will never break it.
Be it exterminating all the dragons inside their lairs, venturing into a domain of extreme danger, or single-handedly overthrowing the territory of a nobleman...
He not only keeps his promises, but does so with finesse and surgical precision. As the Harbingers' vanguard, Childe always appears in the vulnerable underbellies of
Snezhnaya
's enemy states, striking as a conflict is about to erupt.
Character Story 2
Friendship Lv. 3
Many myths in Snezhnaya say that Tartaglia has been on the battlefield since he was 14 years old.
Oddly enough, at that young age he already seemed to be a skilled slaughterer with mastery of many forms of martial arts.
But more terrifying than his skill in combat was his passion for it.
Perilous battles would only excite him, and terrifying foes would exhilarate him all the more.
Childe's arrogance and conceit are derived from honing himself across countless battles, and from unmatched combat experience.
On the other hand, because they fret about the way his quarrelsome personality tends to invite unwanted trouble, the other Harbingers constantly try to get him sent on missions to places as far from Snezhnaya as possible, so that they can avoid the fallout.
The man is incomprehensibly ever in the eye of the storm, and these unusual experiences have also made him quite the show-off who revels in the praise of others.
Unlike other members of the Harbingers, who prefer to keep a low profile, Tartaglia will often go to watch public performances — occasionally even becoming part of the show himself.
Character Story 3
Friendship Lv. 4
Ice fishing has been one of Tartaglia's hobbies since childhood.
In those days, he was known neither by the names Tartaglia nor "Childe," as the Fatui call him, but as Ajax, named by his father after some
hero
's tale. He and his father would cut holes open upon frozen lakes before sitting beside them and tending to their fishing lines.
This was no easy task, and would sometimes take the entire morning.
But whether it was chiseling away at the thick ice or the long waits in between catches, he was always accompanied by his father's unending tales of adventure.
These were stories of his father's adventures from when he was young, and they became the future that Tartaglia secretly envisioned for himself.
So he would pay close attention, putting himself into the shoes of the protagonist of those tales, immersing himself fully in them even as they waited for a fish to bite.
Even after leaving home, Ajax, later to be known as "Childe" Tartaglia, would keep up that hobby of his.
No longer accompanied by the stories of bygone days, fishing instead became an opportunity to train — to hone his endurance, and reflect on combat techniques.
Such long periods of meditation to refine his skills swiftly relegated the question of a successful catch to irrelevance.
Character Story 4
Friendship Lv. 5
Unlike what the rest of the world may think, Tartaglia was not born with his exceptional skills in battle.
But he would never speak to anyone about that critical, fateful encounter of his...
When he was 14, he fled his monotonous life at home with nothing but a shortsword and a bag of bread in hand.
That 14-year-old boy got lost in the snowy forest.
Pursued by bears and wolf packs, he lost his footing and fell into a bottomless crack in the earth's surface.
There, he witnessed the
endless possibilities of another ancient world
. There, he would meet a
mysterious swordswoman
...
Or perhaps one should say that this dark realm had sensed the burning ambition in this boy's heart.
This darkness was something that the Harbinger Childe could never again delve into.
In those three months, the swordswoman taught Tartaglia how to pass through the Abyss unhindered, and more importantly, nurtured the ability to stir up endless havoc from within Ajax's trouble-mongering nature.
No one knew what happened within that darkness during those three months, nor would Ajax ever speak of this to anyone.
When his worried mother and sisters finally found him in that forest, only 3 days had passed in this world.
Thus did Ajax's first adventure end, with his now-rusty shortsword in hand.
This would mark the end of his youth and the beginning of his days as a warrior.
Character Story 5
Friendship Lv. 6
By the time he returned home, the young man was no longer the same.
He was no longer frightened and hesitant but had become frivolous and confident. He acted as if this world revolved around him, and as if battle existed for his sake.
Conflict often brings about change, and this capricious, incalculable change attracted Ajax like a revolving kaleidoscope.
In the eyes of his father, that third son that he had so worried about had changed for the worse, bringing much uncalled-for havoc to the seaside village of
Morepesok
.
Or perhaps it would be more accurate to say that Ajax had become a nexus of strife, for no matter where he went, fights and squabbles would follow — and he reveled in it.
Ultimately, after a huge brawl was pacified with some difficulty and with some near-fatal misses, his father had no choice but to hand his beloved son over for conscription into the Fatui.
He hoped that the strict military training of the Fatui could hone his son's temper, but wound up watching fully-armed troops getting the stuffing beaten out of them by a mere child.
This was a great disappointment to his father, but it also caught the attention of
Pulcinella
, the 5th Harbinger.
Shocked by Ajax's great strength and curious about how he invariably became the eye of a vortex of discord, Pulcinella inducted Ajax into the Fatui under the pretext of meting out punishment, ordering him to start from the bottom and take up the duty of serving the
Tsaritsa
.
His insatiable desire for conquest would thus be constantly satiated in fighting for the Fatui, and his ever-burgeoning ego would gorge upon the elation of vanquishing mighty foes...
Until at last, Ajax was chosen to be one of the Fatui Harbingers, donning the mantle of "Childe" Tartaglia and becoming one of the most powerful people in Snezhnaya.
But becoming Tartaglia was far from his end goal. To someone who sought to conquer the world, this was but a tiny step in his journey.

Delusion
Friendship Lv. 6
Tartaglia's
Delusion
is his old badge of honor and the proof of his strength.
He still remembers with perfect clarity the day when he received his Delusion and ascended to become a member of the Harbingers.
As he stood before the cold, stern Tsaritsa,
The Jester
, the very first of the Fatui, personally pinned this insignia onto him.
It was his reward for slaying many terrible beasts, and a memento of countless battles.
But it was not this that brought Tartaglia joy, for honor is the natural reward of the warrior.
Nor did he pay any mind to the odd looks his new "colleagues" gave him, for the opinions and bellyaches of others meant nothing to him.
The sight of the Tsaritsa on her lofty throne alone stirred the young man's heart with respect and admiration, not merely because she opened the way for him to find an abundance of looming battles, but also because of the way she looked upon him.
Her gaze was cold but pure, arrogant yet sharp.
She was not only the sacrosanct
Cryo Archon
but also a true warrior.
Thus did Childe, having received his Delusion, swear an oath of fealty to the one and only Tsaritsa of Snezhnaya.
