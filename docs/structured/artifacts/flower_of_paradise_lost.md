# Flower of Paradise Lost - Artifact Set

**2-Piece Bonus:** Increases Elemental Mastery by 80.
**4-Piece Bonus:** The equipping character's Bloom, Hyperbloom, and Burgeon reaction DMG are increased by 40%, and their Lunar-Bloom reaction DMG is increased by 10%. Additionally, after the equipping character triggers Bloom, Hyperbloom, Lunar-Bloom, or Burgeon, they will gain another 25% bonus to the effects mentioned prior. Each stack of this lasts 10s. Max 4 stacks simultaneously. This effect can only be triggered once per second. The character who equips this can still trigger its effects when not on the field.
**Flower:** Ay-Khanoum's Myriad
**Plume:** Wilting Feast
**Sands:** A Moment Congealed
**Goblet:** Secret-Keeper's Magic Bottle
**Circlet:** Amethyst Crown
