# Thundering Fury - Artifact Set

**2-Piece Bonus:** Electro DMG Bonus +15%
**4-Piece Bonus:** Increases the DMG caused by Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Superconduct, and Hyperbloom by 40%, the DMG Bonus conferred by Aggravate by 20%, and the DMG caused by Lunar-Charged by 20%. When Quicken or the aforementioned Elemental Reactions are triggered, Elemental Skill CD is decreased by 1s. Can only occur once every 0.8s.
**Flower:** Thunderbird's Mercy
**Plume:** Survivor of Catastrophe
**Sands:** Hourglass of Thunder
**Goblet:** Omen of Thunderstorm
**Circlet:** Thunder Summoner's Crown
